# Peptide Glossary Tool

> This glossary was originally listed on **[Volta Peptides](https://voltapeptides.com)**. See the full glossary at **[voltapeptides.com/glossary](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary)**.

An alphabetical glossary of 438 peptide research terms, covering the basics, preparation, pharmacology, analytical testing, regulatory language, storage and more. Every entry links to its full definition page on Volta Peptides.

## 0-9

**[503A Pharmacy](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/503a-pharmacy)** · *Regulatory*

A compounding pharmacy operating under section 503A of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act, which exempts it from pre-market approval and cGMP provided it compounds against a patient-specific prescription. Oversight is by the state board of pharmacy.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/503a-pharmacy)

**[503B Outsourcing Facility](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/503b-outsourcing-facility)** · *Regulatory*

A compounder registered under section 503B, added by the Drug Quality and Security Act in 2013. Unlike a 503A pharmacy it must follow cGMP, is inspected by the FDA on a risk basis, reports adverse events, and may supply without a patient-specific prescription.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/503b-outsourcing-facility)

## A

**[Accelerated Approval](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/accelerated-approval)** · *Regulatory*

An FDA pathway permitting earlier approval for serious conditions with unmet need, based on a surrogate endpoint believed to predict clinical benefit. Confirmatory trials are still required and the agency can withdraw the approval if benefit is not confirmed.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/accelerated-approval)

**[Acceptance Criterion](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/acceptance-criterion)** · *Quality*

The limit a result is judged against, fixed before the sample is run. A purity specification of not less than 99% is an acceptance criterion; a result of 98.7% fails it whatever the laboratory thinks of the material.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/acceptance-criterion)

**[Accreditation](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/accreditation)** · *Quality*

Formal recognition by an authorised body that a laboratory is competent for specified activities, with a published scope. It is awarded against a standard and is distinct from certification of a management system, which says nothing about technical competence.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/accreditation)

**[Accuracy](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/accuracy)** · *Quality*

How close a measured value sits to the true one. It is established against a reference standard, and it is independent of precision: a method can repeat the same wrong answer all day.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/accuracy)

**[Acetamidomethyl (Acm)](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/acetamidomethyl-acm)** · *Chemistry & Structure*

A cysteine protecting group that survives the acid used for final cleavage. Leaving it on lets disulfide bridges be formed deliberately and in a chosen order rather than at random.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/acetamidomethyl-acm)

**[Acetate Salt](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/acetate-salt)** · *Chemistry & Structure*

The form most synthetic peptides are supplied in, with acetate as the counter-ion. Acetate is preferred over trifluoroacetate for work in cell culture, where residual TFA can be toxic at low concentrations.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/acetate-salt)

**[Acetyl Hexapeptide-8](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/acetyl-hexapeptide-8)** · *Chemistry & Structure*

The INCI name for the six-residue sequence marketed as Argireline. Acetyl hexapeptide-3 appears in older listings for the same material, so two names in a label archive do not mean two ingredients.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/acetyl-hexapeptide-8)

**[Active Ester](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/active-ester)** · *Chemistry & Structure*

A carboxylic acid converted to an ester reactive enough to form an amide bond. It is the intermediate a coupling reagent generates, and how selective that ester is governs how much racemisation occurs.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/active-ester)

**[Active Site](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/active-site)** · *Cell Biology*

The region of an enzyme where substrate binds and catalysis occurs. Inhibitors that occupy it compete directly with substrate, which is a different mechanism from binding elsewhere and changing the shape.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/active-site)

**[Activin Type IIB Receptor (ActRIIB)](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/activin-type-iib-receptor-actriib)** · *Pharmacology*

The receptor through which myostatin and related TGF-beta ligands signal to limit skeletal muscle mass. Decoy receptors and antibodies aimed at it are the main pharmacological route to inhibiting that signal.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/activin-type-iib-receptor-actriib)

**[Acylation](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/acylation)** · *Basics*

Attaching a fatty acid chain so the molecule binds reversibly to serum albumin. The albumin-bound fraction acts as a circulating reservoir and clearance slows from hours to days. GLP-1 SM and cagrilintide both use it.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/acylation)

**[Adduct](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/adduct)** · *Quality*

An ion formed by the analyte plus something else it picked up, commonly sodium or potassium. An unrecognised adduct reads as a mass that does not match the expected one, and as an impurity peak that is not an impurity.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/adduct)

**[Adherence](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/adherence)** · *Evidence & Research*

The extent to which participants followed the assigned protocol. Low adherence pulls a result toward no difference, so it is reported alongside the outcome rather than treated as a footnote.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/adherence)

**[Adverse Analytical Finding](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/adverse-analytical-finding)** · *Anti-Doping*

The formal term for a laboratory report identifying a prohibited substance or its markers in a sample. It opens a results-management process and is not by itself a finding that a rule was broken.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/adverse-analytical-finding)

**[Adverse Event](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/adverse-event)** · *Clinical & Safety*

Any unfavourable medical occurrence in somebody receiving a treatment, whether or not the treatment caused it. The definition is deliberately broad, which is why trial safety tables report events rather than side effects.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/adverse-event)

**[Aggregation](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/aggregation)** · *Storage & Stability*

Peptide molecules associating into clumps under heat, agitation or repeated freezing. Aggregates lose activity, scatter light enough to make a solution look faintly cloudy, and are more immunogenic than the monomer.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/aggregation)

**[Aliquot](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/aliquot)** · *Preparation*

A measured portion divided out of a larger volume. Splitting a reconstituted solution into single-use aliquots is how repeated freeze-thaw of the whole stock is avoided, and it is the standard answer to a material that degrades on repeated freeze-thaw.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/aliquot)

**[Amino Acid](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/amino-acid)** · *Basics*

The unit a peptide is built from. Twenty of them are used to assemble human proteins, each carrying an amino group, a carboxyl group and a distinguishing side chain, and it is the side chains that decide how a finished peptide folds and what it binds.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/amino-acid)

**[Amino Acid Analysis (AAA)](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/amino-acid-analysis-aaa)** · *Quality*

Hydrolysing a peptide back to its constituent amino acids and quantifying each one, which gives composition and total peptide content. It answers a question HPLC does not: how much peptide is actually in the vial.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/amino-acid-analysis-aaa)

**[AMPK (AMP-Activated Protein Kinase)](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/ampk-amp-activated-protein-kinase)** · *Signaling Pathways*

The cell's energy sensor, activated when the AMP to ATP ratio rises under exercise or fasting, shifting metabolism toward glucose uptake, fat oxidation, mitochondrial biogenesis and autophagy. MOTS-c activates it; AICAR does so indirectly through its intracellular metabolite ZMP and is a nucleoside rather than a peptide.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/ampk-amp-activated-protein-kinase)

**[Amylin](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/amylin)** · *Pharmacology*

A hormone co-secreted with insulin by pancreatic beta cells, which slows gastric emptying, suppresses glucagon and promotes satiety. Pramlintide is a synthetic analogue; cagrilintide is a long-acting acylated one.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/amylin)

**[Analogue](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/analogue)** · *Basics*

A known peptide altered on purpose, by substituting a residue, shortening the chain or attaching something to it, so that it resists degradation or binds differently while still acting at the same target. GLP-1 SM is an analogue of GLP-1.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/analogue)

**[Analyte](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/analyte)** · *Quality*

The substance a method is set up to measure, as distinct from the sample it sits in and from everything else the detector sees. A purity method has the peptide as its analyte; a residual solvent method run on the same vial has a different one.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/analyte)

**[Analytical Method](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/analytical-method)** · *Quality*

The written procedure a laboratory follows to produce a result: sample preparation, instrument conditions, how the signal is turned into a number and what counts as a valid run. Two laboratories reporting different purities for one lot are usually running different methods rather than disagreeing.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/analytical-method)

**[Analytical Selectivity](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/analytical-selectivity)** · *Quality*

A method's ability to measure the intended analyte without interference from impurities, excipients or degradation products. A purity figure from a method that cannot separate a close-eluting impurity overstates purity.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/analytical-selectivity)

**[Anaphylaxis](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/anaphylaxis)** · *Clinical & Safety*

A severe systemic allergic reaction with onset in seconds to minutes, affecting airway, breathing and circulation. It is rare with peptides, and the first-line emergency treatment in the literature is epinephrine.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/anaphylaxis)

**[Angiogenesis](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/angiogenesis)** · *Pharmacology*

The growth of new blood vessels from vessels that already exist. It is central to tissue repair and is the mechanism most often proposed for the tissue-repair research peptides, almost entirely on the strength of animal models.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/angiogenesis)

**[Anhydrous Basis](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/anhydrous-basis)** · *Quality*

A result recalculated as though the water content had been removed, using a separately measured water figure. Two certificates can disagree by several percent purely because one reports anhydrous and the other as received.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/anhydrous-basis)

**[Anti-Drug Antibodies (ADA)](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/anti-drug-antibodies-ada)** · *Clinical & Safety*

Antibodies raised against a peptide or protein given in an animal or clinical study. Neutralising ADA can cancel the effect of a biologic entirely, so assays for them are part of the standard safety package.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/anti-drug-antibodies-ada)

**[Apoptosis](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/apoptosis)** · *Cell Biology*

Programmed cell death: an orderly self-destruct a cell runs when it is damaged or surplus, in contrast to the inflammatory mess of necrosis. Senolytics work by restoring it in senescent cells that have become resistant.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/apoptosis)

**[Area Under the Curve (AUC)](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/area-under-the-curve-auc)** · *Pharmacology*

Total exposure over time, the integral of plasma concentration against time. Two regimens can share a peak concentration and differ substantially in AUC, which is why trials report both.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/area-under-the-curve-auc)

**[As-Received Basis](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/as-received-basis)** · *Quality*

A result calculated on the material exactly as it arrived, water and counterion included. It is the honest basis for what is in the vial and reads lower than the same measurement on a dry basis.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/as-received-basis)

**[Aseptic Technique](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/aseptic-technique)** · *Preparation*

The handling practices that keep a preparation from being contaminated: disinfecting a stopper before it is pierced, using a fresh needle, and not touching the parts of the equipment that meet the solution. It governs contamination, not sterility, which a container either had or did not.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/aseptic-technique)

**[Aspartimide Formation](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/aspartimide-formation)** · *Chemistry & Structure*

A side reaction at aspartic acid where the side chain cyclises onto the backbone, generating a family of related impurities. Certain neighbouring residues make it much more likely, and it is one of the main reasons a sequence proves hard to synthesise cleanly.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/aspartimide-formation)

**[Assay](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/assay)** · *Quality*

Any procedure that measures whether something is present, how much of it there is, or how active it is. Analytical assays report composition; functional assays report activity, and a material can pass one and fail the other.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/assay)

**[Athlete Biological Passport](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/athlete-biological-passport)** · *Anti-Doping*

A longitudinal record of an athlete's own blood and steroid markers, used to detect doping indirectly by flagging departures from that individual's baseline rather than by detecting a substance at all.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/athlete-biological-passport)

**[Audit Trail](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/audit-trail)** · *Quality*

The instrument's own tamper-evident log of acquisitions, reprocessing and deletions, with the user and timestamp attached. It is what distinguishes a reported result from the only result obtained.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/audit-trail)

**[Autophagy](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/autophagy)** · *Cell Biology*

The process by which a cell encloses its own damaged proteins and organelles and digests them, recycling the parts. Fasting and AMPK activation both raise it, and it is one of the better-established links between energy state and maintenance.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/autophagy)

## B

**[Batch Number](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/batch-number)** · *Quality*

The identifier tying a quantity of material to the records of its manufacture and testing. It is the only thing linking a vial in hand to a certificate on a screen, which is why a certificate whose batch number does not appear on the label proves nothing about that vial.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/batch-number)

**[BDNF (Brain-Derived Neurotrophic Factor)](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/bdnf-brain-derived-neurotrophic-factor)** · *Signaling Pathways*

A neurotrophin supporting neuronal survival, dendritic growth and synaptic plasticity. It is the most frequently cited mechanism for nootropic peptide claims and is usually measured in rodents, which is the main limitation on reading those claims across to humans.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/bdnf-brain-derived-neurotrophic-factor)

**[Beyond-Use Date (BUD)](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/beyond-use-date-bud)** · *Storage & Stability*

The date after which a compounded preparation should no longer be used, set by the compounder from applicable stability data. It is not an expiry date, which is assigned to a manufactured product from its own stability programme.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/beyond-use-date-bud)

**[Binding Affinity](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/binding-affinity)** · *Pharmacology*

How tightly a molecule holds its receptor, reported as a dissociation constant where a lower number means tighter binding. Affinity alone does not predict the size of a response: that also depends on how well the bound molecule activates the receptor.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/binding-affinity)

**[Bioactive Peptide](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/bioactive-peptide)** · *Chemistry & Structure*

A peptide that produces a measurable response in living cells, as distinct from one that is merely present. The term is used loosely across food science, topical formulation and pharmacology, so it carries no implication about how strong the evidence is.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/bioactive-peptide)

**[Bioavailability](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/bioavailability)** · *Pharmacology*

The fraction of an administered amount that reaches the circulation intact and is therefore available to act. Intravenous delivery is 100% by definition; everything else is measured against it.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/bioavailability)

**[Biochemical Pathway](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/biochemical-pathway)** · *Cell Biology*

A linked series of reactions converting one molecule into another, each step usually enzyme-catalysed. Describing a compound as acting on a pathway says where it intervenes, not what the whole system does in response.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/biochemical-pathway)

**[Biomarker](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/biomarker)** · *Evidence & Research*

A measurable indicator standing in for a process, a disease state or a response. Serum IGF-1 is the usual biomarker in growth hormone secretagogue work, and a biomarker moving is not the same as an outcome improving.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/biomarker)

**[Bioregulator](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/bioregulator)** · *Basics*

A very short peptide, typically two to four residues, from a line of work begun in Russia by Khavinson and colleagues and based on fragments reported in specific tissues. The published evidence is largely Russian-language and the group has little independent replication.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/bioregulator)

**[Biosimilar](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/biosimilar)** · *Regulatory*

A biological product shown to be highly similar to an approved reference biologic with no clinically meaningful differences. It is not a generic: biologics cannot be manufactured identically, so similarity has to be demonstrated rather than assumed.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/biosimilar)

**[Biosynthesis](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/biosynthesis)** · *Cell Biology*

Production of a molecule by a living system rather than by chemical synthesis. Recombinant expression in bacterial or mammalian cells is the biosynthetic route to peptides too long to assemble stepwise.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/biosynthesis)

**[Black Box Warning](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/black-box-warning)** · *Clinical & Safety*

The most serious warning the FDA can require on a label, set in a box at the top for risks that can lead to death or serious injury. GLP-1 receptor agonists carry one for thyroid C-cell tumours, on the basis of rodent findings.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/black-box-warning)

**[Blinding](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/blinding)** · *Evidence & Research*

Withholding treatment allocation from participants, investigators or outcome assessors. It fails in practice whenever a compound has obvious effects, which is a known weakness of trials in this area.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/blinding)

**[Blood-Brain Barrier (BBB)](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/blood-brain-barrier-bbb)** · *Cell Biology*

The tight endothelial lining of brain capillaries, which keeps most large and charged molecules out of brain tissue. Whether a peptide crosses it is usually the deciding question for any proposed central effect, which is why so much central work in the literature uses an intranasal route.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/blood-brain-barrier-bbb)

**[Boc (tert-Butyloxycarbonyl)](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/boc-tert-butyloxycarbonyl)** · *Chemistry & Structure*

An acid-labile protecting group, and the basis of the older synthesis strategy that preceded Fmoc. Boc chemistry requires repeated acid treatment and a harsh final cleavage, which is why it survives mainly for sequences Fmoc handles badly.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/boc-tert-butyloxycarbonyl)

**[Body Composition](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/body-composition)** · *Pharmacology*

How body weight divides between fat mass and fat-free mass. Two interventions can produce the same change in total weight with very different composition behind it, which is why studies increasingly report the split rather than the total alone.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/body-composition)

**[Body Mass Index (BMI)](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/body-mass-index-bmi)** · *Clinical & Safety*

Weight in kilograms divided by height in metres squared, used in trials as an entry criterion and a reported characteristic. It does not distinguish one tissue type from another, which is why body composition is measured separately.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/body-mass-index-bmi)

**[Boxed Warning](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/boxed-warning)** · *Regulatory*

The most serious warning the FDA requires on a product label, set apart in a box. It describes a risk identified for an approved product and is part of the label rather than a statement about a substance in general.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/boxed-warning)

**[Buffer](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/buffer)** · *Preparation*

A solution that resists pH change when acid or base is added. Peptide stability and solubility both track pH, so a buffered diluent keeps a preparation in a known state instead of a drifting one.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/buffer)

**[Bulk Drug Substance](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/bulk-drug-substance)** · *Regulatory*

The active pharmaceutical ingredient used as a starting material in compounding. Under 503A it may be used only if it meets a USP or NF monograph, is a component of an approved drug, or appears on the FDA's 503A bulks list, and it must arrive with a certificate from a registered manufacturer.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/bulk-drug-substance)

## C

**[Calibration](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/calibration)** · *Quality*

Relating an instrument's signal to known quantities of a reference standard, so a peak area can be read as a concentration. An uncalibrated instrument still produces numbers; they just do not mean anything outside that run.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/calibration)

**[Capping](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/capping)** · *Chemistry & Structure*

Permanently blocking any chain that failed to couple, so it cannot react in later cycles. It converts a deletion impurity that would be hard to separate into a short truncated one that is easier to remove.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/capping)

**[Carbodiimide](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/carbodiimide)** · *Chemistry & Structure*

A class of coupling reagent including DIC and EDC, usually paired with an additive to suppress side reactions. They are inexpensive and widely used, and the by-product they form differs in how easily it washes away.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/carbodiimide)

**[Cardiolipin](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/cardiolipin)** · *Cell Biology*

A phospholipid found almost only in the inner mitochondrial membrane, where the respiratory complexes depend on it. Damage to it is tied to ageing and metabolic disease, and SS-31 is studied because it binds cardiolipin selectively.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/cardiolipin)

**[Cardiovascular Outcomes Trial](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/cardiovascular-outcomes-trial)** · *Evidence & Research*

A large trial powered to detect differences in events such as myocardial infarction, stroke and cardiovascular death rather than in a laboratory marker. It is the design regulators require before a cardiovascular benefit may be claimed.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/cardiovascular-outcomes-trial)

**[Carrier Peptide](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/carrier-peptide)** · *Chemistry & Structure*

A class from the topical-formulation literature whose members bind a trace metal and deliver it, copper being the usual one. GHK-Cu is the canonical example, and the copper is a cofactor for enzymes involved in matrix assembly rather than an inert passenger.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/carrier-peptide)

**[CAS Number](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/cas-number)** · *Quality*

The identifier the Chemical Abstracts Service assigns to a specific substance, such as 137525-51-0 for BPC-157. It pins identity down in a way a trade name or an internal code cannot, and a compound genuinely without one is not the same thing as a missing field.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/cas-number)

**[CAS Registry Number](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/cas-registry-number)** · *Chemistry & Structure*

A unique identifier assigned to a chemical substance by the Chemical Abstracts Service. It is specific to the exact substance, so a free base and its salt carry different numbers, and many research peptides have never been assigned one.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/cas-registry-number)

**[Case Report and Case Series](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/case-report-and-case-series)** · *Evidence & Research*

A published description of one patient, or a handful, with no control group. Useful for flagging a rare harm or an unexpected observation, and not evidence that something works.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/case-report-and-case-series)

**[Cell Membrane](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/cell-membrane)** · *Cell Biology*

The lipid bilayer enclosing a cell, which controls what enters and leaves. Its hydrophobic interior is the reason large polar molecules such as peptides need a receptor or a transporter rather than simply diffusing in.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/cell-membrane)

**[Certificate of Analysis (COA)](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/certificate-of-analysis-coa)** · *Quality*

The analytical report for one batch, stating identity, purity and whatever else was tested. Its worth depends entirely on who ran the tests and on whether the batch number matches the material in hand, which is what makes a batch number worth checking rather than glancing at.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/certificate-of-analysis-coa)

**[Certificate of Origin](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/certificate-of-origin)** · *Regulatory*

A trade document stating the country where goods were produced, used by customs to apply the correct treatment. It describes provenance and makes no statement about composition, purity or quality.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/certificate-of-origin)

**[cGMP (Current Good Manufacturing Practice)](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/cgmp-current-good-manufacturing-practice)** · *Quality*

The regulations governing how licensed pharmaceutical manufacturers must produce and control a product, covering facilities, records, personnel and validation. They apply to drug manufacture and not to research-grade supply, which is a distinction worth keeping straight.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/cgmp-current-good-manufacturing-practice)

**[Chain of Custody](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/chain-of-custody)** · *Quality*

The documented record of who held a sample, when, and in what condition, from collection to analysis. Without it a result describes a sample whose history cannot be shown, which matters most when the result is disputed.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/chain-of-custody)

**[Charge State](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/charge-state)** · *Quality*

How many charges an ion carries in the mass spectrometer. Peptides commonly appear in several at once, and the series of peaks they produce is what allows the true molecular mass to be calculated.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/charge-state)

**[Chromatogram](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/chromatogram)** · *Quality*

The detector trace a chromatographic run produces: signal against time, with each component appearing as a peak. The purity figure on a certificate is arithmetic performed on the areas of those peaks.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/chromatogram)

**[Chromatography](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/chromatography)** · *Quality*

Any technique that separates a mixture by how its components distribute between a moving phase and a stationary one. Reversed-phase liquid chromatography is the version peptide work almost always means.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/chromatography)

**[Class II Recall](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/class-ii-recall)** · *Regulatory*

The FDA recall class for a product whose use may cause temporary or medically reversible harm, with a remote probability of serious consequences. Every peptide-related recall to date has fallen in this class.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/class-ii-recall)

**[Clearance](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/clearance)** · *Pharmacology*

The rate at which the body removes a compound from blood, through the kidneys, the liver, or enzymatic breakdown in plasma. Clearance together with volume of distribution is what produces the half-life, rather than the other way round.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/clearance)

**[Cleavage](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/cleavage)** · *Chemistry & Structure*

Releasing the finished chain from the resin, usually with trifluoroacetic acid, which also strips most side-chain protection in the same step. Scavengers are included to catch the reactive fragments this generates.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/cleavage)

**[Clinical Trial](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/clinical-trial)** · *Evidence & Research*

A study testing an intervention in human volunteers under a protocol fixed before enrolment. The protocol, not the published paper, is what defines the question the trial was built to answer.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/clinical-trial)

**[ClinicalTrials.gov](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/clinicaltrials-gov)** · *Evidence & Research*

The US National Library of Medicine's public registry of clinical studies. Comparing a registered protocol against the published paper is how a switched endpoint is caught.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/clinicaltrials-gov)

**[Co-elution](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/co-elution)** · *Quality*

Two components leaving the column together and arriving as one peak. It is the main way a purity figure can be honestly measured and still be too high, and it is why an orthogonal method is worth running.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/co-elution)

**[Cochrane Review](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/cochrane-review)** · *Evidence & Research*

A systematic review published by Cochrane, an international network with published methodology and conflict-of-interest rules. Cochrane reviews are generally treated as among the more rigorous syntheses available.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/cochrane-review)

**[Cold Chain](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/cold-chain)** · *Preparation*

The unbroken temperature-controlled path a material travels from manufacture to storage. Lyophilised peptide is typically held at -20C and solutions at 2 to 8C, and a break in the chain is not visible in the vial afterwards, which is why shipping records matter.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/cold-chain)

**[Collagen](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/collagen)** · *Cell Biology*

The most abundant structural protein in skin, tendon and bone, a triple helix rich in glycine and proline. Published findings for skin-matrix peptides generally rest on measured collagen synthesis by fibroblasts.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/collagen)

**[Compounded Medication](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/compounded-medication)** · *Regulatory*

A preparation made up by a licensed pharmacy for a specific patient's requirements. It sits under a different regulatory regime from a manufactured drug, with different expectations about testing, labelling and shelf life.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/compounded-medication)

**[Compounding](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/compounding)** · *Regulatory*

Preparing a customised medicine by combining or altering ingredients, done by a licensed pharmacy or an outsourcing facility. Compounded drugs are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed for safety, effectiveness or quality before they are supplied.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/compounding)

**[Concentration](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/concentration)** · *Preparation*

How much peptide sits in a unit of liquid, written as mg/mL or mcg/mL. It is a consequence of the vial contents and the solvent volume added, and nothing else, which is why it has to be recalculated whenever either changes.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/concentration)

**[Confidence Interval](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/confidence-interval)** · *Evidence & Research*

The range of values compatible with the data at a stated level, usually 95%. A wide interval means an imprecise estimate however striking the central figure looks, and it is the more honest number to read first.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/confidence-interval)

**[Contraindication](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/contraindication)** · *Clinical & Safety*

A circumstance in which a treatment should not be used. An absolute contraindication rules it out entirely; a relative one means the balance has to be weighed. Both are statements found on a label.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/contraindication)

**[Controlled Substances Schedule](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/controlled-substances-schedule)** · *Regulatory*

The classification placing a substance under legal control according to accepted medical use and potential for abuse, with different lists in each country. Most research peptides sit outside these schedules entirely, which is a separate question from whether they are approved.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/controlled-substances-schedule)

**[Copper Tripeptide-1](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/copper-tripeptide-1)** · *Chemistry & Structure*

The INCI name for GHK-Cu, the glycyl-L-histidyl-L-lysine copper complex. The two names denote one molecule, and which appears depends on whether the document is an ingredient label or a research paper.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/copper-tripeptide-1)

**[Cortisol](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/cortisol)** · *Pharmacology*

The adrenal glucocorticoid released under stress and on a daily rhythm. It is measured in growth hormone secretagogue studies because some compounds in that class raise it transiently while others do not, and that difference is what selectivity means here.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/cortisol)

**[Counter-Ion](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/counter-ion)** · *Chemistry & Structure*

The small ion supplied alongside a charged peptide to balance it, usually acetate and sometimes trifluoroacetate. It is part of the powder's mass, so gross vial weight and peptide content are two different numbers.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/counter-ion)

**[Coupling Reagent](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/coupling-reagent)** · *Chemistry & Structure*

The activator that turns a carboxylic acid into a form reactive enough to form an amide bond with the next amino group. Coupling efficiency compounds over a synthesis, so a small per-step loss becomes a large one over a long sequence.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/coupling-reagent)

**[CREB (cAMP Response Element-Binding Protein)](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/creb-camp-response-element-binding-protein)** · *Signaling Pathways*

A transcription factor switched on downstream of cAMP, central to synaptic plasticity, memory formation and neuronal survival. Semax and Dihexa are studied for effects on CREB-dependent gene expression.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/creb-camp-response-element-binding-protein)

**[Cristae](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/cristae)** · *Cell Biology*

The inward folds of the inner mitochondrial membrane, which hold the electron transport chain and ATP synthase. How tightly folded they are sets how much respiratory machinery a mitochondrion can carry, so cristae architecture is an efficiency measure.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/cristae)

**[Crossover Trial](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/crossover-trial)** · *Evidence & Research*

A design in which each participant receives both treatments in sequence with a washout between, so each person acts as their own control. It needs fewer participants and only suits stable, reversible conditions.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/crossover-trial)

**[Cyclization](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/cyclization)** · *Basics*

Closing a peptide into a ring, head to tail or through a side-chain bridge. The ring has fewer conformations to lose and no free ends for exopeptidases to attack, which is why so many peptide drugs are cyclic.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/cyclization)

**[Cysteine](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/cysteine)** · *Chemistry & Structure*

The amino acid whose thiol side chain forms disulfide bridges. It is also one of the three residues most readily oxidised, so a sequence containing it has a predictable degradation liability.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/cysteine)

**[Cytokine](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/cytokine)** · *Cell Biology*

A small secreted protein cells use to signal to one another, prominent in immune communication. Pro-inflammatory cytokines such as TNF-alpha and IL-6 are the standard readout in anti-inflammatory peptide studies.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/cytokine)

## D

**[D-Retro-Inverso](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/d-retro-inverso)** · *Basics*

A design in which the sequence is read backwards and every L-amino acid is swapped for its D isomer. Side-chain geometry comes out close to the original while proteases, which recognise L-residues, no longer cut it. FOXO4-DRI is built this way.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/d-retro-inverso)

**[DAC (Drug Affinity Complex)](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/dac-drug-affinity-complex)** · *Basics*

A maleimidopropionic acid linker that couples to albumin after administration. On CJC-1295 it takes the half-life from minutes to roughly eight days, which is the whole difference between the DAC and no-DAC versions of that compound.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/dac-drug-affinity-complex)

**[DailyMed](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/dailymed)** · *Regulatory*

The US National Library of Medicine's database of current FDA drug labelling, and the canonical place a prescribing information document is retrieved from rather than quoted secondhand.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/dailymed)

**[Dalton (Da)](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/dalton-da)** · *Chemistry & Structure*

The unit of molecular mass, one twelfth the mass of a carbon-12 atom. Peptides are quoted in daltons and proteins usually in kilodaltons, and the figure on a certificate is the monoisotopic or average mass depending on the method.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/dalton-da)

**[Deamidation](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/deamidation)** · *Storage & Stability*

A degradation route in which an asparagine or glutamine side chain loses its amide group, often via a cyclic intermediate that can also rearrange the backbone. Heat and alkaline pH speed it up and it shows on a chromatogram as a close-running impurity.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/deamidation)

**[Decision Rule](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/decision-rule)** · *Quality*

The stated convention for how measurement uncertainty is taken into account when calling a result a pass or a fail. Simple acceptance ignores it; a guarded rule requires the result to clear the limit by a margin.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/decision-rule)

**[Deconvolution](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/deconvolution)** · *Quality*

The arithmetic that turns a series of multiply-charged peaks back into one molecular mass. It is the step between what the instrument recorded and the single number printed on a certificate.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/deconvolution)

**[Degradation Product](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/degradation-product)** · *Quality*

A compound formed as a peptide breaks down, through oxidation, deamidation, hydrolysis or aggregation. Certificates report these as impurity peaks, and their pattern says more about how a batch was handled than the headline purity figure does.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/degradation-product)

**[Denaturation](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/denaturation)** · *Chemistry & Structure*

Loss of folded structure through heat, extreme pH or mechanical shear, without the backbone necessarily being cut. The mass can be unchanged while the activity is gone, which is why an identity test alone does not establish that a material is intact.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/denaturation)

**[Deprotection](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/deprotection)** · *Chemistry & Structure*

Removing a protecting group to expose the site underneath. In Fmoc synthesis it names the piperidine step that frees the amino group for the next coupling, repeated once per residue.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/deprotection)

**[Desensitization](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/desensitization)** · *Pharmacology*

A fall in receptor responsiveness under prolonged or repeated stimulation, achieved by internalising receptors or expressing fewer of them. It is one mechanism behind an effect that fades while exposure continues.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/desensitization)

**[Desiccant](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/desiccant)** · *Storage & Stability*

A moisture-absorbing material, usually silica gel, packed alongside a dry product to keep ambient humidity away from it. Residual moisture is what drives hydrolysis in a powder, so the sachet is doing real work.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/desiccant)

**[Dietary Supplement](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/dietary-supplement)** · *Regulatory*

A US regulatory category for products intended to supplement the diet, which are not reviewed for safety or effectiveness before sale. Substances investigated as drugs are generally excluded from it, which is why peptides do not belong in that category.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/dietary-supplement)

**[Diluent](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/diluent)** · *Preparation*

The liquid used to dissolve or further dilute a peptide. Buffered saline, dilute acetic acid and small fractions of organic solvent all appear in the literature, chosen for what the compound will dissolve in and what the assay will tolerate.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/diluent)

**[Dimerisation](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/dimerisation)** · *Chemistry & Structure*

Two molecules associating or bonding into one unit. For peptides it most often means an unintended disulfide-linked dimer, which appears as a distinct peak at twice the expected mass.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/dimerisation)

**[Dipeptide](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/dipeptide)** · *Chemistry & Structure*

Two residues joined by a single peptide bond. Di- and tripeptides are the only chain lengths the intestinal PepT1 transporter carries intact, which is why they come up in discussions of oral absorption.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/dipeptide)

**[Dissociation Constant (Kd)](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/dissociation-constant-kd)** · *Pharmacology*

The concentration at which half the available binding sites are occupied at equilibrium. A lower value means tighter binding, and unlike IC50 it describes the interaction rather than the readout used to measure it.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/dissociation-constant-kd)

**[Disulfide Bond](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/disulfide-bond)** · *Chemistry & Structure*

A covalent bridge between the sulfur atoms of two cysteine residues. It staples distant parts of a chain together and holds a fold in place, and reducing conditions break it, which is one way a peptide loses activity without losing mass.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/disulfide-bond)

**[Dose-Response Curve](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/dose-response-curve)** · *Pharmacology*

A plot of effect against amount administered. A clean curve is among the stronger indications that an observed effect is real rather than noise, and the gap between the amount that first works and the amount that harms is the therapeutic window.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/dose-response-curve)

**[Double-Blind](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/double-blind)** · *Evidence & Research*

Neither participants nor investigators know who is in which arm, so expectation cannot colour reported symptoms or outcome assessment. It is a design feature, not a guarantee, and papers increasingly report whether blinding held.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/double-blind)

**[Downregulation](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/downregulation)** · *Pharmacology*

A reduction in the number of receptors a cell presents, in response to sustained stimulation. It is slower than desensitization and is the usual reason a protocol in the literature includes periods without administration.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/downregulation)

**[Downstream Effect](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/downstream-effect)** · *Signaling Pathways*

A consequence occurring later in a pathway than the point a compound acts on. The further downstream an observation sits, the more other inputs have had a chance to influence it.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/downstream-effect)

**[DPP-4 (Dipeptidyl Peptidase-4)](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/dpp-4-dipeptidyl-peptidase-4)** · *Pharmacology*

The enzyme that inactivates native GLP-1 and GIP within a couple of minutes of their release by clipping two residues off the N-terminus. Long-acting incretin analogues are engineered to resist it, which is what turns a minutes-long hormone into a weekly one.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/dpp-4-dipeptidyl-peptidase-4)

**[Drug Identification Number (DIN)](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/drug-identification-number-din)** · *Regulatory*

The eight-digit number Health Canada assigns to a product authorised for sale in Canada. Its presence means a specific product has been reviewed and authorised; research chemicals carry none.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/drug-identification-number-din)

**[Drug Interaction](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/drug-interaction)** · *Clinical & Safety*

A change in one drug's effect caused by another drug, by food or by a supplement. Compounds that slow gastric emptying can alter how fast an oral medicine is absorbed, which is a pharmacokinetic interaction rather than a pharmacodynamic one.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/drug-interaction)

**[Drug Shortage Declaration](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/drug-shortage-declaration)** · *Regulatory*

An FDA finding that a drug is in shortage, which temporarily widens what compounders may prepare. The GLP-1 SM shortage was declared resolved in February 2025 and GLP-2 TZ in October 2024, which closed that allowance for both.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/drug-shortage-declaration)

**[Dual Agonist](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/dual-agonist)** · *Pharmacology*

One molecule that activates two receptors. GLP-2 TZ is a GLP-1 and GIP agonist; survodutide combines GLP-1 with glucagon-receptor activity. The pairing is a design decision about which pathways to engage at once.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/dual-agonist)

## E

**[EC50](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/ec50)** · *Pharmacology*

The concentration producing half of a compound's maximum effect in a given assay. It is a potency measure and is only comparable between compounds tested in the same system, which is why cross-paper EC50 comparisons mislead so often.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/ec50)

**[Effect Size](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/effect-size)** · *Evidence & Research*

How large the difference an intervention produced actually was, in real units or as a standardised measure. Once significance is established this is the only question left that matters.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/effect-size)

**[Elastin](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/elastin)** · *Cell Biology*

The matrix protein that lets skin and vessel walls stretch and recoil. It is laid down mostly early in life and replaced poorly afterwards, which is why its loss is harder to reverse than collagen's.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/elastin)

**[Electrospray Ionisation (ESI)](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/electrospray-ionisation-esi)** · *Quality*

The soft ionisation technique that gets peptides into a mass spectrometer intact, by spraying the solution through a charged needle. It tends to produce several charge states of one molecule rather than a single ion.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/electrospray-ionisation-esi)

**[EMA (European Medicines Agency)](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/ema-european-medicines-agency)** · *Regulatory*

The European Union agency that evaluates medicines for the EU market. Its scientific opinions lead to authorisations granted by the European Commission, so approval status in Europe can differ from the FDA's for the same molecule.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/ema-european-medicines-agency)

**[Endogenous and Exogenous](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/endogenous-and-exogenous)** · *Basics*

Endogenous describes a molecule the organism makes itself, such as native GLP-1 or growth hormone. Exogenous describes one introduced from outside. The distinction matters in assay work, because an exogenous analogue and the endogenous hormone it mimics can both be present in a sample.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/endogenous-and-exogenous)

**[Endoplasmic Reticulum (ER)](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/endoplasmic-reticulum-er)** · *Cell Biology*

The folded membrane network where secreted and membrane proteins are assembled and folded, and where lipids are made. A targeting sequence is what routes a nascent protein into it.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/endoplasmic-reticulum-er)

**[Endotoxin](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/endotoxin)** · *Quality*

Lipopolysaccharide from the outer membrane of Gram-negative bacteria. It survives ordinary sterilisation and is not removed by filtration alone, and it provokes a febrile inflammatory response, which is why it is assayed separately from sterility.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/endotoxin)

**[Endpoint](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/endpoint)** · *Evidence & Research*

A defined, measurable outcome a trial uses to judge an intervention. Which endpoints were chosen, and when, is as informative as the results reported against them.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/endpoint)

**[Energy Expenditure](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/energy-expenditure)** · *Pharmacology*

Total energy used over a day, made up of resting metabolism, the thermic effect of food and physical activity. Glucagon-receptor activity is one of the few peptide mechanisms proposed in trials to raise it directly rather than by reducing intake.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/energy-expenditure)

**[Enteric Coating](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/enteric-coating)** · *Preparation*

A polymer film on a capsule or tablet that holds together in stomach acid and dissolves at the higher pH of the small intestine. Oral peptide formulations use it to get past gastric proteases, with limited success for anything but short sequences.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/enteric-coating)

**[Enzymatic Degradation](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/enzymatic-degradation)** · *Pharmacology*

Cleavage of a peptide by proteases in blood and tissue, which cut at sequence-specific points. Designing around it, by substituting a residue, cyclising the chain or capping a terminus, is the central problem in making a natural peptide usable as a drug.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/enzymatic-degradation)

**[Enzyme](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/enzyme)** · *Cell Biology*

A protein that catalyses a reaction, raising its rate without being consumed. Enzymes build peptides and also destroy them, which is why proteases appear on both sides of peptide science.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/enzyme)

**[Enzyme-Inhibitor Peptide](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/enzyme-inhibitor-peptide)** · *Chemistry & Structure*

A class from the topical-formulation literature that works by slowing an enzyme rather than by signalling, usually a matrix metalloproteinase that degrades collagen. The mechanism is conservation of existing matrix rather than synthesis of new matrix.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/enzyme-inhibitor-peptide)

**[Epigenetics](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/epigenetics)** · *Cell Biology*

Changes in gene expression that do not alter the DNA sequence, such as methylation and histone modification. They are heritable through cell division and are one route by which a transient exposure has a lasting effect.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/epigenetics)

**[Epimerisation](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/epimerisation)** · *Chemistry & Structure*

Loss of stereochemistry at one centre, converting an L-residue to its D-form during synthesis. The product has the same mass and often a very similar retention time, so it is an impurity ordinary purity testing can miss.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/epimerisation)

**[Estimand](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/estimand)** · *Evidence & Research*

A precise statement of what a trial is estimating: the population, the endpoint, and how events such as discontinuation are handled. Two analyses of one dataset can differ because they answer different estimands rather than because one is wrong.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/estimand)

**[Excipient](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/excipient)** · *Preparation*

Anything in a formulation other than the active substance: bulking agents, buffers, preservatives, stabilisers. Excipients decide how a cake looks and how well it redissolves, and they appear on an approved label alongside the active.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/excipient)

**[Experimental Model](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/experimental-model)** · *Evidence & Research*

The system a finding was produced in: a cell line, an isolated tissue, an animal strain, or human participants. It bounds what the finding supports, and the gap between a cultured cell and an organism is where most translation failures occur.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/experimental-model)

**[Expiry Date](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/expiry-date)** · *Storage & Stability*

The date after which material is no longer considered to meet its specification. Unlike a retest date it is not extended by a further test, and it is only meaningful alongside the storage conditions it was established under.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/expiry-date)

**[Expression of Concern](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/expression-of-concern)** · *Evidence & Research*

A notice a journal attaches to a paper when it has credible doubts about its integrity but has not finished investigating. It is a warning short of retraction and is easy to miss on a citation.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/expression-of-concern)

**[Extracellular Matrix](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/extracellular-matrix)** · *Cell Biology*

The scaffold of collagen, elastin, proteoglycans and glycoproteins that cells sit in and attach to. Repair is a matter of remodelling it, so matrix turnover is the common language of wound and skin research.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/extracellular-matrix)

## F

**[Fasting Plasma Glucose](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/fasting-plasma-glucose)** · *Clinical & Safety*

Blood glucose measured after a defined period without food, a standard endpoint in metabolic trials. It describes one moment, which is why it is usually reported alongside HbA1c.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/fasting-plasma-glucose)

**[FDA Approval](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/fda-approval)** · *Regulatory*

A finding by the US Food and Drug Administration that a drug's benefits outweigh its risks for a specific labelled use. Approval is always for named indications and never for a substance in general, so a molecule can be approved for one use and investigational for every other.

**[FDA Category 1](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/fda-category-1)** · *Regulatory*

In the FDA's review of substances nominated for compounding, the list of those evaluated and found eligible for use as bulk drug substances by 503A and 503B compounders.

**[FDA Category 2](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/fda-category-2)** · *Regulatory*

The list of nominated substances the FDA has flagged with significant safety risks, which are therefore not eligible for compounding while the nomination stands. BPC-157 was placed in this category.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/fda-category-2)

**[FDA Warning Letter](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/fda-warning-letter)** · *Regulatory*

A published notice telling a company the agency believes it has broken the law, and requiring a written response. Warning letters are public and searchable, which makes them usable primary evidence about a supplier rather than hearsay.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/fda-warning-letter)

**[Fibroblast](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/fibroblast)** · *Cell Biology*

The connective-tissue cell that produces collagen and the rest of the extracellular matrix. Most skin and wound-model peptide research is a measurement of what fibroblasts do in culture rather than a clinical outcome.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/fibroblast)

**[First-Pass Metabolism](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/first-pass-metabolism)** · *Pharmacology*

The breakdown a swallowed compound undergoes in the gut wall and the liver before it reaches general circulation. It is a principal reason peptide drug development has concentrated on parenteral routes, which bypass the gut.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/first-pass-metabolism)

**[Fmoc (Fluorenylmethyloxycarbonyl)](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/fmoc-fluorenylmethyloxycarbonyl)** · *Chemistry & Structure*

The base-labile protecting group that dominates modern solid-phase peptide synthesis, removed with piperidine between couplings. It gives the strategy its name: Fmoc chemistry builds the chain under mild conditions no strong acid step is needed for.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/fmoc-fluorenylmethyloxycarbonyl)

**[Follicle-Stimulating Hormone (FSH)](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/follicle-stimulating-hormone-fsh)** · *Pharmacology*

The other pituitary gonadotropin, which supports ovarian follicle development and spermatogenesis. It is released alongside LH under GnRH control, and the two are usually measured together.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/follicle-stimulating-hormone-fsh)

**[Freeze-Thaw Cycle](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/freeze-thaw-cycle)** · *Storage & Stability*

One round of freezing and thawing, during which ice crystal growth and the concentration of solutes at the freezing front push peptide molecules together. Repeated rounds degrade a material measurably, and aliquoting is the standard way to avoid them.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/freeze-thaw-cycle)

## G

**[Gallbladder Disease](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/gallbladder-disease)** · *Clinical & Safety*

Gallstones and cholecystitis, which appear more often in trials of GLP-1 receptor agonists than on placebo. The usual explanation is the rapid reduction in body weight participants in those studies undergo, which is itself a gallstone risk factor independently of any drug.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/gallbladder-disease)

**[Gastric Emptying](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/gastric-emptying)** · *Pharmacology*

How fast stomach contents pass into the small intestine. GLP-1 and amylin signalling slow it, which blunts post-meal glucose rises and prolongs fullness, and which also accounts for much of the nausea recorded in incretin trials.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/gastric-emptying)

**[Gastroparesis](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/gastroparesis)** · *Clinical & Safety*

Markedly delayed gastric emptying, with nausea, vomiting and early fullness. It is a recognised consequence of GLP-1 receptor agonist treatment and follows directly from the mechanism, which is slowing gastric motility.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/gastroparesis)

**[Ghrelin](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/ghrelin)** · *Pharmacology*

A stomach-derived hormone that drives appetite and, by way of the growth hormone secretagogue receptor, triggers growth hormone release. The GHRP family and several non-peptide agonists of the same receptor are studied as mimics of it.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/ghrelin)

**[GHRH (Growth Hormone Releasing Hormone)](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/ghrh-growth-hormone-releasing-hormone)** · *Pharmacology*

The hypothalamic hormone that instructs the pituitary to synthesise and release growth hormone. Sermorelin, tesamorelin and CJC-1295 are analogues of it, and they act at a different receptor from the GHRPs, which is the basis for studying the two together.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/ghrh-growth-hormone-releasing-hormone)

**[GHRP (Growth Hormone Releasing Peptide)](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/ghrp-growth-hormone-releasing-peptide)** · *Pharmacology*

A family of synthetic peptides that release growth hormone by activating the ghrelin receptor. GHRP-2, GHRP-6, hexarelin and ipamorelin belong to it, and they differ in how much hunger, cortisol and prolactin signalling they bring with them.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/ghrp-growth-hormone-releasing-peptide)

**[GHS-R1a (Growth Hormone Secretagogue Receptor)](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/ghs-r1a-growth-hormone-secretagogue-receptor)** · *Pharmacology*

The ghrelin receptor, a G protein-coupled receptor expressed mainly in the pituitary and hypothalamus. Activation by ghrelin or by a GHRP produces a growth hormone pulse along with appetite and reward signalling.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/ghs-r1a-growth-hormone-secretagogue-receptor)

**[GIP (Glucose-Dependent Insulinotropic Polypeptide)](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/gip-glucose-dependent-insulinotropic-polypeptide)** · *Pharmacology*

The second incretin, released from the upper small intestine, which also enhances glucose-dependent insulin secretion. It is a target of GLP-2 TZ alongside GLP-1 and of GLP-3 RT alongside GLP-1 and glucagon.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/gip-glucose-dependent-insulinotropic-polypeptide)

**[GLP-1 (Glucagon-Like Peptide-1)](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/glp-1-glucagon-like-peptide-1)** · *Pharmacology*

An incretin released from the small intestine after a meal. It raises insulin secretion when glucose is high, suppresses glucagon, slows gastric emptying and signals fullness centrally. Its native half-life is about two minutes.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/glp-1-glucagon-like-peptide-1)

**[GLP-1 Receptor](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/glp-1-receptor)** · *Pharmacology*

The G protein-coupled receptor GLP-1 acts at, expressed in pancreatic islets, the stomach, the heart and several brain regions. The breadth of that expression is why agonists at it affect more than blood glucose.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/glp-1-receptor)

**[GLP-1 Receptor Agonist](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/glp-1-receptor-agonist)** · *Pharmacology*

A compound that activates the GLP-1 receptor. GLP-1 SM and GLP-1 LR are the approved examples, studied against glycaemic endpoints and against body weight, and the class is the reference point most newer incretin-receptor peptides are compared with.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/glp-1-receptor-agonist)

**[Glucagon](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/glucagon)** · *Pharmacology*

The pancreatic alpha-cell hormone that raises blood glucose through glycogenolysis and gluconeogenesis, and that also increases energy expenditure. Adding glucagon-receptor activity to an incretin agonist is what dual agonists such as survodutide do.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/glucagon)

**[Glycaemic Control](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/glycaemic-control)** · *Clinical & Safety*

How closely blood glucose is held within a target range over time, assessed from HbA1c, fasting glucose and postprandial measurements together. It is the composite endpoint most incretin trials are designed around.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/glycaemic-control)

**[Glycosylation](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/glycosylation)** · *Chemistry & Structure*

Attachment of sugar chains to a protein, typically at asparagine or at serine and threonine. It affects stability, solubility and clearance, and it is a reason a recombinant molecule may differ from the same sequence made synthetically.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/glycosylation)

**[GMP Grade](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/gmp-grade)** · *Quality*

Material produced under Good Manufacturing Practice, a regulated system covering facilities, documentation and release rather than a purity figure. It is a claim about how material was made, and it is auditable.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/gmp-grade)

**[GnRH (Gonadotropin-Releasing Hormone)](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/gnrh-gonadotropin-releasing-hormone)** · *Pharmacology*

The hypothalamic hormone released in pulses that tells the pituitary to secrete luteinising hormone and follicle-stimulating hormone. Continuous rather than pulsatile stimulation of its receptor has the opposite effect and shuts those hormones down.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/gnrh-gonadotropin-releasing-hormone)

**[Golgi Apparatus](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/golgi-apparatus)** · *Cell Biology*

The stack of membranes that finishes proteins arriving from the ER, adding or trimming sugars and sorting them to their destinations. Many peptide hormones are cut from a larger precursor on the way through it.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/golgi-apparatus)

**[GRAS](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/gras)** · *Regulatory*

Generally Recognized As Safe, a United States designation for food substances supported by expert consensus or long use. It concerns use in food and carries no implication for any other context.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/gras)

**[Grey Market](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/grey-market)** · *Regulatory*

Supply that is legal somewhere but moves outside the channel a regulated medicine would travel through. The practical consequence is that chain of custody, storage history and identity cannot be verified by the buyer.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/grey-market)

**[Growth Hormone (GH)](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/growth-hormone-gh)** · *Pharmacology*

The pituitary hormone released in pulses that influences growth, body composition and metabolism. Most of its longer-term effects are mediated indirectly, through IGF-1 produced in the liver in response to it.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/growth-hormone-gh)

**[Growth Hormone Secretagogue (GHS)](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/growth-hormone-secretagogue-ghs)** · *Pharmacology*

Any compound that makes the pituitary release its own growth hormone rather than supplying growth hormone itself. Ipamorelin, the GHRPs and non-peptide GHS-R1a agonists all fall in the class, and so do the GHRH analogues by a different receptor.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/growth-hormone-secretagogue-ghs)

**[Guard Band](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/guard-band)** · *Quality*

The margin a decision rule holds back from a specification limit to absorb measurement uncertainty. It makes a false pass less likely at the cost of failing material that may in fact conform.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/guard-band)

## H

**[Half-Life](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/half-life)** · *Pharmacology*

The time taken for the amount in the body to fall by half. It sets how often a compound has to be given and how long elimination takes, about four to five half-lives for the bulk of it to clear.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/half-life)

**[Hazard Ratio](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/hazard-ratio)** · *Evidence & Research*

The ratio of the rate at which an event occurs in the treatment arm to the rate in the control arm over time. Below 1 favours treatment, and an interval crossing 1 has not demonstrated a difference.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/hazard-ratio)

**[HbA1c (Glycated Hemoglobin)](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/hba1c-glycated-hemoglobin)** · *Evidence & Research*

A blood measure reflecting average glucose over roughly the preceding two to three months, reported as a percentage. It is the standard primary endpoint in metabolic trials, which is why a result quoted in points of HbA1c appears in almost every incretin paper.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/hba1c-glycated-hemoglobin)

**[Health Canada](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/health-canada)** · *Regulatory*

The federal department that authorises therapeutic products for the Canadian market and issues a Drug Identification Number to each one. Research-use material is not a therapeutic product and carries no DIN, which is the distinction that governs how peptides may be supplied in Canada.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/health-canada)

**[HOBt (Hydroxybenzotriazole)](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/hobt-hydroxybenzotriazole)** · *Chemistry & Structure*

A coupling additive that forms a more selective active ester, raising yield and suppressing racemisation. Its shipping restrictions as a dry solid are part of why alternatives such as Oxyma came into use.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/hobt-hydroxybenzotriazole)

**[Homeostasis](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/homeostasis)** · *Cell Biology*

The maintenance of internal conditions within a narrow range by opposing regulatory mechanisms. It is why a system perturbed in one direction usually produces a compensating response rather than a simple linear change.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/homeostasis)

**[HPLC (High-Performance Liquid Chromatography)](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/hplc-high-performance-liquid-chromatography)** · *Quality*

The separation technique behind the purity figure on a certificate. A sample is pushed through a packed column under pressure and the components come off at different times, so a 99% result means 99% of what the detector saw was the target peak.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/hplc-high-performance-liquid-chromatography)

**[HPLC-UV](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/hplc-uv)** · *Quality*

High-performance liquid chromatography with an ultraviolet detector, typically reading at 214 nm where the peptide bond absorbs. It reports how much of the absorbing material is the target peak, which is a purity measurement and not an identity one.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/hplc-uv)

**[hs-CRP](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/hs-crp)** · *Clinical & Safety*

High-sensitivity C-reactive protein, a blood marker of systemic inflammation measured by an assay able to resolve the low end of the range. It is non-specific: a raised value indicates inflammation without indicating its source.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/hs-crp)

**[Hydrolysis](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/hydrolysis)** · *Storage & Stability*

Cleavage of the peptide backbone by water, which proceeds slowly in solution and faster at extremes of pH or temperature. It is the reason a lyophilised powder outlasts the solution made from it by a wide margin.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/hydrolysis)

**[Hydrophilic and Hydrophobic](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/hydrophilic-and-hydrophobic)** · *Chemistry & Structure*

Whether a region of a molecule associates with water or avoids it. The balance across a sequence sets solubility, how a peptide behaves on a reversed-phase column, and how readily it crosses a lipid membrane.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/hydrophilic-and-hydrophobic)

**[Hydrophilicity](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/hydrophilicity)** · *Chemistry & Structure*

Affinity for water. In peptides it is set by the balance of polar and charged residues, and together with its opposite it governs solubility, how the chain folds, and where it elutes in reversed-phase chromatography.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/hydrophilicity)

**[Hypothalamus](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/hypothalamus)** · *Pharmacology*

The brain region joining the nervous and endocrine systems, releasing GHRH, GnRH and somatostatin to control the pituitary. It also carries much of the appetite circuitry that incretin agonists act on.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/hypothalamus)

## I

**[IC50](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/ic50)** · *Pharmacology*

The concentration producing half of the maximum inhibition in a given assay. It is assay-dependent rather than a constant of the compound, so IC50 values are comparable only when the conditions behind them are.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/ic50)

**[IGF-1 (Insulin-Like Growth Factor 1)](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/igf-1-insulin-like-growth-factor-1)** · *Pharmacology*

A liver-produced hormone that mediates much of what growth hormone does to growth, metabolism and body composition. Serum IGF-1 is the usual laboratory proxy for growth hormone exposure because it is stable where GH itself is pulsatile.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/igf-1-insulin-like-growth-factor-1)

**[Immunogenicity](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/immunogenicity)** · *Clinical & Safety*

The tendency of an administered peptide or protein to provoke an antibody response. Trials report it routinely, because antibodies can blunt a drug's effect and, less often, cause reactions of their own.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/immunogenicity)

**[In Vitro](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/in-vitro)** · *Evidence & Research*

In glassware: cells, tissue or isolated enzymes outside a living organism. Concentrations used in vitro are frequently unreachable in a body, which caps what such a result can predict.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/in-vitro)

**[In Vivo](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/in-vivo)** · *Evidence & Research*

In a living organism, animal or human. In-vivo work accounts for absorption, distribution, metabolism and clearance in a way cell work cannot, which is why an in-vitro effect so often disappears in vivo.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/in-vivo)

**[In-Competition and Out-of-Competition Testing](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/in-competition-and-out-of-competition-testing)** · *Anti-Doping*

Samples collected in the window around a competition, as against at any other time and usually unannounced. The distinction decides which classes of the Prohibited List apply to a given sample.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/in-competition-and-out-of-competition-testing)

**[INCI Name](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/inci-name)** · *Quality*

The International Nomenclature of Cosmetic Ingredients name, which is the spelling required on ingredient labels in the EU and used on them in most other markets. One molecule therefore carries two names: GHK-Cu is Copper Tripeptide-1, and Matrixyl is Palmitoyl Pentapeptide-4.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/inci-name)

**[Incretin](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/incretin)** · *Pharmacology*

A gut hormone released after eating that raises insulin secretion in a glucose-dependent way, meaning it acts when glucose is high and not when it is low. GLP-1 and GIP are the two in humans.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/incretin)

**[IND (Investigational New Drug)](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/ind-investigational-new-drug)** · *Regulatory*

The FDA application that allows an unapproved substance to be given to humans in a clinical trial. Most research peptides have no IND behind them, which is precisely why they cannot be marketed for human use.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/ind-investigational-new-drug)

**[Indication](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/indication)** · *Regulatory*

The condition and population a medicine is approved to be used in, as written in its labelling. It is the boundary that makes the difference between approved use and off-label use.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/indication)

**[Insulin](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/insulin)** · *Pharmacology*

The pancreatic beta-cell hormone that moves glucose out of the blood into muscle, liver and fat. It is a 51-residue two-chain peptide held together by disulfide bridges, and it was the first peptide hormone made recombinantly.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/insulin)

**[Insulin Resistance](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/insulin-resistance)** · *Pharmacology*

A state in which muscle, liver and fat respond poorly to insulin, so more of it is needed to keep glucose in range. It is the physiological backdrop to most metabolic endpoints reported in this field.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/insulin-resistance)

**[Insulin Sensitivity](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/insulin-sensitivity)** · *Pharmacology*

The converse measure: how strongly tissues respond to a given amount of insulin. Clamp studies are the reference method and surrogate indices derived from fasting bloods are what most papers actually report.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/insulin-sensitivity)

**[Intention-to-Treat](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/intention-to-treat)** · *Evidence & Research*

Analysing every participant in the arm they were randomised to, whether or not they finished. It preserves the randomisation and usually gives a more conservative answer than analysing completers only.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/intention-to-treat)

**[International Unit (IU)](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/international-unit-iu)** · *Preparation*

A unit of biological activity rather than of mass, defined separately for each substance against an agreed reference preparation. HCG and somatropin are quoted in IU, so an IU figure cannot be turned into milligrams without the conversion specific to that compound.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/international-unit-iu)

**[Investigational Drug](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/investigational-drug)** · *Regulatory*

A substance under study that no regulator has approved for sale. In the United States it may be given to people only under an Investigational New Drug application. Most compounds supplied as research peptides are investigational or have never entered human study at all.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/investigational-drug)

**[ISO/IEC 17025](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/iso-iec-17025)** · *Quality*

The international standard for the competence of testing and calibration laboratories. Accreditation to it is granted by a named body for a defined scope, so a laboratory can be accredited for one test and not another.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/iso-iec-17025)

**[Isoelectric Point (pI)](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/isoelectric-point-pi)** · *Chemistry & Structure*

The pH at which a peptide carries no net charge. Solubility is usually at its worst there, so a material that will not dissolve in a neutral buffer often dissolves once the pH is moved away from its pI.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/isoelectric-point-pi)

**[Isomer](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/isomer)** · *Chemistry & Structure*

A compound with the same molecular formula as another but a different arrangement of atoms. Isomers share a mass, so mass spectrometry alone cannot separate them and chromatography or a chiral method is required.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/isomer)

## K

**[Kaiser Test](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/kaiser-test)** · *Chemistry & Structure*

A colour test using ninhydrin that detects free primary amines on resin beads, used to confirm a coupling went to completion. A blue result means unreacted amine remains and the coupling should be repeated.

What a mass spectrometer actually measures: mass divided by charge, not mass. A 4,000 Da peptide carrying three charges appears near m/z 1,334, which is why a raw spectrum rarely shows the molecular weight directly.

**[Melanocortin Receptor](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/melanocortin-receptor)** · *Pharmacology*

**[Method Verification](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/method-verification)** · *Quality*

Demonstrating that an already-validated method performs as expected in the laboratory now using it. A compendial method arrives validated; a laboratory still has to show its own instruments and analysts reproduce it.

An unbroken chain of comparisons linking a result back to a recognised reference, each step with its own stated uncertainty. It is what allows two laboratories using different instruments to be talking about the same milligram.

The United Kingdom regulator for medicines and medical devices. It classifies most therapeutic peptides as prescription-only medicines, a category that governs supply rather than possession.

One millionth of a gram, written mcg or ug, with 1,000 of them to the milligram. Peptide quantities move between the two scales constantly, and a factor of a thousand is the easiest arithmetic error to make when reading a vial against a published protocol.

**[Mitochondria](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/mitochondria)** · *Cell Biology*

The organelles that generate most of a cell's ATP through oxidative phosphorylation. They carry their own small genome, which is where the mitochondrial-derived peptides are encoded.

**[Mitochondrial Biogenesis](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/mitochondrial-biogenesis)** · *Cell Biology*

Building new mitochondria, which raises a cell's aerobic capacity. Endurance exercise is its best-established trigger; MOTS-c and AICAR are studied for driving it pharmacologically through AMPK.

**[Mitochondrial-Derived Peptide](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/mitochondrial-derived-peptide)** · *Chemistry & Structure*

A short peptide encoded within mitochondrial DNA rather than in the nucleus, MOTS-c and humanin being the best known. Their existence is why the mitochondrial genome is now discussed as a signalling genome and not only a respiratory one.

**[Molar Ratio](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/molar-ratio)** · *Chemistry & Structure*

In a blend, the proportion of one compound to another counted in molecules rather than in milligrams. Two blends with the same mass ratio have different molar ratios whenever the molecular weights differ, which they almost always do.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/molar-ratio)

**[Molarity](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/molarity)** · *Preparation*

Concentration counted in moles per litre rather than in mass per volume. Receptor and enzyme work is specified in molarity because a binding site counts molecules, and converting from mg/mL requires the molecular weight.

**[Molecular Formula](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/molecular-formula)** · *Chemistry & Structure*

The count of each element in one molecule, written as a string such as C221H342N46O68. It fixes the molecular mass exactly, which is why a formula and a stated mass that disagree indicate an error in one of them.

**[Molecular Mass](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/molecular-mass)** · *Chemistry & Structure*

The mass of one molecule, in daltons. The average mass uses the natural mixture of isotopes and the monoisotopic mass uses the lightest of each, and the two diverge by several daltons on a peptide of any size.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/molecular-mass)

**[Molecular Weight](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/molecular-weight)** · *Chemistry & Structure*

The mass of one molecule, in daltons or g/mol. For a peptide it follows arithmetically from the sequence, so a stated weight that does not match the stated sequence is a contradiction on the page and a reason to look harder at the rest of the document.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/molecular-weight)

A protein secreted by muscle that acts as a brake on its own growth, also called GDF-8. Blocking it increases muscle mass in rodent models, which is why follistatin and ACE-031 have been studied, and why the clinical record for the approach is a run of failures.

## O

**[Observational Study](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/observational-study)** · *Evidence & Research*

Research that records what happens without assigning the intervention, in cohort or case-control form. It shows association at a scale trials cannot reach and can never rule out that the groups differed to begin with.

**[Open-Label](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/open-label)** · *Evidence & Research*

**[Orthogonal Protection](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/orthogonal-protection)** · *Chemistry & Structure*

Using protecting groups removed by different chemistry, so one can be taken off while others stay on. It is what makes selective modification possible, including branched and cyclic structures.

**[Osmolarity](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/osmolarity)** · *Preparation*

The total concentration of dissolved particles in a solution. Preparations used on living cells are matched to physiological osmolarity, because a hypotonic or hypertonic medium stresses the cells independently of whatever is being tested.

**[Out of Specification (OOS)](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/out-of-specification-oos)** · *Quality*

A result that falls outside an acceptance criterion. It triggers an investigation into whether the measurement or the material is at fault, and an OOS cannot be resolved by simply retesting until a passing number appears.

**[Oxidation](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/oxidation)** · *Storage & Stability*

Reaction of methionine, cysteine or tryptophan side chains with oxygen, accelerated by light, air and trace peroxides. It costs activity, and a sequence containing none of those three residues has no oxidation liability to begin with.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/oxidation)

An imbalance in which reactive oxygen species outrun a cell's antioxidant defences and begin damaging lipids, proteins and DNA. Mitochondria are both the main source and a main casualty, which is why mitochondrial peptides are measured against it.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/oxidative-stress)

**[Palmitoyl Pentapeptide-4](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/palmitoyl-pentapeptide-4)** · *Chemistry & Structure*

The INCI name for the palmitoylated five-residue sequence sold as Matrixyl. The palmitoyl group is a lipid tail added to help a hydrophilic peptide associate with the stratum corneum.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/palmitoyl-pentapeptide-4)

**[Pancreatitis](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/pancreatitis)** · *Clinical & Safety*

Inflammation of the pancreas, presenting with severe abdominal pain and raised lipase. It is monitored as a potential adverse event across the incretin class and appears in the labels of approved GLP-1 receptor agonists.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/pancreatitis)

**[Partial Agonist](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/partial-agonist)** · *Pharmacology*

An agonist that cannot produce the full response however much of it is present, because it activates the receptor less completely than the natural ligand does. Against a full agonist it can behave as a brake.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/partial-agonist)

**[Parts Per Million (ppm)](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/parts-per-million-ppm)** · *Quality*

One milligram per kilogram, or one microgram per gram. Residual solvents and elemental impurities are reported this way, and mass spectrometers also quote mass accuracy in ppm, which is a different use of the same unit.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/parts-per-million-ppm)

**[PCAC (Pharmacy Compounding Advisory Committee)](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/pcac-pharmacy-compounding-advisory-committee)** · *Regulatory*

The FDA advisory committee that reviews substances nominated for the compounding bulks lists, weighing the physicochemical characterisation, safety data and historical use before recommending for or against inclusion under 503A and 503B.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/pcac-pharmacy-compounding-advisory-committee)

**[Peak Integration](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/peak-integration)** · *Quality*

Deciding where a peak starts and stops so its area can be calculated. Where the baseline is drawn changes the number, which is why integration parameters belong in the method rather than in the analyst's judgement on the day.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/peak-integration)

**[Peer Review](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/peer-review)** · *Evidence & Research*

Assessment of a manuscript by independent researchers before a journal publishes it. It catches some errors and does not verify that the underlying data exist or are correct.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/peer-review)

**[PEGylation](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/pegylation)** · *Basics*

Attaching polyethylene glycol chains to a peptide. The added hydrodynamic bulk slows glomerular filtration and shields the backbone from enzymes, so the molecule circulates for longer. PEG-MGF carries the name of the modification.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/pegylation)

**[Pentadecapeptide](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/pentadecapeptide)** · *Chemistry & Structure*

Fifteen residues. BPC-157 is routinely described this way in the literature, so the word is more common in peptide writing than its length would suggest.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/pentadecapeptide)

**[Pentapeptide](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/pentapeptide)** · *Chemistry & Structure*

Five residues. The prefix is a residue count and nothing more, which is worth saying because ingredient names in topical formulations lean on it heavily.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/pentapeptide)

**[PepT1 Transporter](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/pept1-transporter)** · *Pharmacology*

The proton-coupled transporter SLC15A1 in the intestinal epithelium, which carries di- and tripeptides across intact. It is the one established route by which a very short oral peptide can be absorbed without being broken down first; longer chains cannot use it.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/pept1-transporter)

**[Peptide](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/peptide)** · *Basics*

A chain of amino acids joined by peptide bonds, conventionally fewer than about 50 of them. The cut-off between a peptide and a protein is a convention rather than a chemical boundary, and several compounds supplied for research sit either side of it.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/peptide)

**[Peptide Blend](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/peptide-blend)** · *Preparation*

One vial holding two or more compounds pre-mixed at a fixed ratio by the supplier, as against a set of single-compound vials, where each compound has its own vial. A blend's certificate reports each component separately and states no single purity for the vial.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/peptide-blend)

**[Peptide Bond](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/peptide-bond)** · *Basics*

The amide link between two amino acids, formed when the carboxyl group of one condenses with the amino group of the next and a molecule of water is lost. A run of these bonds is the backbone that makes a molecule a peptide.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/peptide-bond)

**[Peptide Fragment](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/peptide-fragment)** · *Chemistry & Structure*

A section cut from a longer peptide or protein and studied on the assumption that it carries part of the parent's activity. The assumption is not automatic: a fragment can retain binding and lose function, or do neither. HGH Fragment 176-191 is named for the residues it spans.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/peptide-fragment)

**[Peptide Hormone](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/peptide-hormone)** · *Chemistry & Structure*

A peptide the body releases into circulation to act on distant tissue. Insulin, glucagon, growth hormone and GLP-1 are all peptide hormones, and many of the research compounds in this field are analogues of one of them.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/peptide-hormone)

**[Peptidomimetic](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/peptidomimetic)** · *Chemistry & Structure*

A molecule built to reproduce what a peptide does without being one, often by replacing amide bonds with linkages proteases cannot cut. The point is stability and absorption rather than novelty of target.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/peptidomimetic)

**[pH](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/ph)** · *Preparation*

The acidity of a solution on a logarithmic scale. It determines what charge a peptide carries, how soluble it is, and how fast deamidation and hydrolysis proceed, which makes it a stability variable rather than a detail.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/ph)

**[Pharmacodynamics](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/pharmacodynamics)** · *Pharmacology*

What a compound does to the body: the link between concentration at the target and the size of the resulting effect. It is the counterpart to pharmacokinetics and the two are routinely modelled together.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/pharmacodynamics)

**[Pharmacokinetics](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/pharmacokinetics)** · *Pharmacology*

What the body does to a compound: absorption, distribution, metabolism and excretion. Half-life, peak concentration and clearance all come out of pharmacokinetic work.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/pharmacokinetics)

**[Pharmacovigilance](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/pharmacovigilance)** · *Clinical & Safety*

The ongoing collection and assessment of safety information after a drug is on the market, where rare events that a trial of a few thousand participants could never have detected first appear.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/pharmacovigilance)

**[Phase I Trial](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/phase-i-trial)** · *Evidence & Research*

First administration in humans, typically 20 to 100 volunteers, looking at safety, tolerability and pharmacokinetics rather than at whether the compound works. Most research peptides have never reached this stage.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/phase-i-trial)

**[Phase II Trial](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/phase-ii-trial)** · *Evidence & Research*

Up to several hundred participants with the condition in question, testing whether there is an effect worth confirming and finding the amount to carry forward. It is where most candidates fail.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/phase-ii-trial)

**[Phase III Trial](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/phase-iii-trial)** · *Evidence & Research*

The pivotal study, commonly 300 to 3,000 participants or more, powered on a pre-specified endpoint and comparing against placebo or standard care. Approval decisions rest on it.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/phase-iii-trial)

**[Phase IV Trial](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/phase-iv-trial)** · *Evidence & Research*

Study after approval, in ordinary clinical use rather than in a selected trial population. Long-term and rare harms surface here, which is why an approval is not the end of the evidence.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/phase-iv-trial)

**[PI3K/AKT/mTOR Pathway](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/pi3k-akt-mtor-pathway)** · *Signaling Pathways*

The cascade through which growth factors including IGF-1 and insulin control growth, metabolism, survival and protein synthesis. Anabolic peptides act through it. Inhibiting mTOR, as rapamycin does, is a separate longevity strategy pointing the other way along the same pathway.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/pi3k-akt-mtor-pathway)

**[Pilot Study](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/pilot-study)** · *Evidence & Research*

A small preliminary study run to test feasibility and gather estimates for designing a real one. Pilot results are routinely over-read as findings in their own right, which they are not.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/pilot-study)

**[Piperidine](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/piperidine)** · *Chemistry & Structure*

The base used to remove the Fmoc group, usually as a 20% solution in DMF. It appears once per residue in a standard synthesis, and the by-product it forms is monitored to confirm each deprotection happened.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/piperidine)

**[Pituitary Gland](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/pituitary-gland)** · *Pharmacology*

The gland at the base of the brain that releases growth hormone, the gonadotropins and other hormones on hypothalamic instruction. Secretagogues and releasing-hormone analogues both act here rather than at the tissue where the effect appears.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/pituitary-gland)

**[Placebo](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/placebo)** · *Evidence & Research*

An inactive preparation made to look like the active one, given to the comparison arm. Placebo groups routinely improve, which is exactly why an uncontrolled result cannot be read as evidence of an effect.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/placebo)

**[Placebo Effect](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/placebo-effect)** · *Evidence & Research*

Improvement that follows the expectation of treatment rather than the treatment itself. It is real, measurable and occasionally large, and separating it from a drug effect is the entire purpose of a control arm.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/placebo-effect)

**[Placebo-Controlled](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/placebo-controlled)** · *Evidence & Research*

A design in which the comparison arm receives placebo rather than nothing or another active agent, isolating the intervention's own contribution to whatever changed.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/placebo-controlled)

**[Polypeptide](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/polypeptide)** · *Chemistry & Structure*

A chain long enough that counting residues stops being useful, conventionally above about fifty, at which point most writers switch to calling it a protein.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/polypeptide)

**[Pooled Sample](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/pooled-sample)** · *Quality*

Material combined from several containers and tested as one. It reports the average and hides variation between them, so a pooled result says less about any individual vial than its purity figure suggests.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/pooled-sample)

**[Post-Translational Modification](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/post-translational-modification)** · *Chemistry & Structure*

Any chemical change made to a chain after it is assembled: acetylation, amidation, phosphorylation, glycosylation and others. The same sequence with and without one is two different molecules with two different masses.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/post-translational-modification)

**[Postprandial](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/postprandial)** · *Clinical & Safety*

Occurring after a meal. Postprandial glucose and lipid measurements describe the response to a challenge, which is a different question from the fasting value and often a more sensitive one.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/postprandial)

**[Potency](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/potency)** · *Pharmacology*

How little of a compound is needed to produce a given effect. A more potent molecule works at a lower concentration, which says nothing about whether its maximum achievable effect is larger.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/potency)

**[Precision](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/precision)** · *Quality*

How close repeated measurements of the same sample fall to each other, reported as a relative standard deviation. Repeatability is precision within one run, intermediate precision across days and analysts in one laboratory.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/precision)

**[Preclinical](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/preclinical)** · *Evidence & Research*

Work done before any human administration: cell culture, tissue and animal models. Most peptides discussed in this field have preclinical evidence only, and rodent findings fail to carry across to humans far more often than they succeed.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/preclinical)

**[Prescribing Information](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/prescribing-information)** · *Regulatory*

The approved label for a medicine, carrying its indications, administration schedule, contraindications and warnings. It is the authoritative statement of what a drug is approved to do, and it is public.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/prescribing-information)

**[Primary Endpoint](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/primary-endpoint)** · *Evidence & Research*

The single outcome a trial was designed and powered to answer, fixed before data collection. Secondary endpoints are informative and are not what the trial succeeded or failed on, a distinction that press releases regularly blur.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/primary-endpoint)

**[Prohibited List](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/prohibited-list)** · *Anti-Doping*

WADA's list of substances and methods banned in sport, revised every year and organised into numbered classes. Some classes are prohibited at all times, others only in competition.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/prohibited-list)

**[Prolactin](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/prolactin)** · *Pharmacology*

A pituitary hormone best known for its role in lactation, also measured in secretagogue studies because several GHRPs raise it modestly. Ipamorelin's reputation rests largely on not doing so.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/prolactin)

**[Prostaglandin](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/prostaglandin)** · *Pharmacology*

A family of locally acting lipid mediators derived from arachidonic acid, involved in inflammation, pain, smooth-muscle tone and blood flow. They are lipids rather than peptides and appear here because peptide studies measure them as readouts.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/prostaglandin)

**[Protecting Group](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/protecting-group)** · *Chemistry & Structure*

A chemical group fitted to a reactive site so it stays out of the way while a reaction happens elsewhere, then removed. Peptide synthesis is largely the management of these: every amino acid side chain that could react has one.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/protecting-group)

**[Protein](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/protein)** · *Basics*

A long amino acid chain, usually above the 50-residue convention, that folds into a defined three-dimensional structure. Collagen, insulin and antibodies are proteins; the shorter chains studied as research peptides are not, although the chemistry of the backbone is identical.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/protein)

**[Protein Folding](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/protein-folding)** · *Chemistry & Structure*

The process by which a chain adopts its three-dimensional shape, driven mainly by burying hydrophobic residues away from water. Activity usually depends on the fold, which is why a denatured molecule can be chemically intact and inert.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/protein-folding)

**[Pseudoproline](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/pseudoproline)** · *Chemistry & Structure*

A reversibly modified serine or threonine dipeptide that disrupts chain aggregation during synthesis and reverts at cleavage. It is one of the standard tools for a sequence that otherwise fails partway through.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/pseudoproline)

**[Publication Bias](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/publication-bias)** · *Evidence & Research*

The tendency for positive results to be published while null and negative ones are not, which leaves the visible literature skewed toward effects. In a field with few large trials it is the single largest distortion.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/publication-bias)

**[PubMed](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/pubmed)** · *Evidence & Research*

The US National Library of Medicine's free index of biomedical literature, covering more than 30 million citations. A reference that resolves to a PubMed, PMC or DOI record can be checked; one that does not, cannot.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/pubmed)

**[Pulsatile Release](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/pulsatile-release)** · *Pharmacology*

Secretion in bursts rather than at a steady level. Growth hormone and GnRH are both pulsatile, and the pattern carries information: continuous stimulation of a GnRH receptor suppresses the axis it would otherwise drive.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/pulsatile-release)

**[Purity](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/purity)** · *Quality*

The proportion of a sample that is the intended peptide rather than related impurities, measured chromatographically and quoted as a percentage. It says nothing about identity, sterility or endotoxin content, each of which is a separate test. Volta releases every batch against a >99% HPLC specification.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/purity)

## Q

**[Quality Control (QC)](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/quality-control-qc)** · *Quality*

The testing a batch passes through before release, typically identity by mass spectrometry, purity by HPLC, content and, where relevant, water, sterility and endotoxin. QC is the set of tests; the certificate is the report of their results.

## R

**[Racemisation](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/racemisation)** · *Chemistry & Structure*

Loss of stereochemical purity toward an equal mixture of both forms. Activation chemistry is where peptides are most at risk, which is why coupling additives that suppress it are standard rather than optional.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/racemisation)

**[Randomized Controlled Trial (RCT)](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/randomized-controlled-trial-rct)** · *Evidence & Research*

A study in which participants are allocated to arms by chance, so that known and unknown differences between groups even out. It is the strongest single-study design for showing that an intervention caused an outcome.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/randomized-controlled-trial-rct)

**[Reagent Blank](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/reagent-blank)** · *Quality*

Everything in the analysis except the sample, carried through the whole procedure. It shows what the solvents, glassware and column contribute, so that signal is not read as something in the material.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/reagent-blank)

**[Receptor](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/receptor)** · *Cell Biology*

A protein, on the cell surface or inside it, that binds a specific molecule and converts that binding into a change in what the cell does. Which receptors a peptide binds, and how tightly, decides nearly everything about its behaviour.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/receptor)

**[Receptor Agonist](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/receptor-agonist)** · *Pharmacology*

A molecule that binds a receptor and switches it on, producing the kind of response the endogenous signal produces. Most peptide drugs are agonists at a receptor their parent hormone already occupies.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/receptor-agonist)

**[Receptor Antagonist](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/receptor-antagonist)** · *Pharmacology*

A molecule that occupies a receptor without activating it, so the natural ligand cannot bind. The effect is to suppress a pathway rather than drive it.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/receptor-antagonist)

**[Receptor Selectivity](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/receptor-selectivity)** · *Pharmacology*

How far a compound acts on its intended receptor rather than on related ones. Selectivity is the difference between a GHRP that raises growth hormone alone and one that also moves cortisol and prolactin.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/receptor-selectivity)

**[Recombinant](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/recombinant)** · *Basics*

Produced by an engineered organism rather than by chemical synthesis. A gene is expressed in bacterial or mammalian cells and the product is purified from the culture. Longer chains such as somatropin are made this way; short peptides are usually synthesised instead.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/recombinant)

**[Reconstitution](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/reconstitution)** · *Preparation*

Returning a freeze-dried peptide to solution by adding a measured volume of solvent. The volume chosen is what sets the concentration, so one vial can yield many different concentrations and the arithmetic belongs with the record of the experiment.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/reconstitution)

**[Reference Standard](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/reference-standard)** · *Quality*

A well-characterised sample run alongside an unknown so that retention time and response can be compared against something known. Without one, a chromatogram shows separation but supports no identification.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/reference-standard)

**[REMS (Risk Evaluation and Mitigation Strategy)](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/rems-risk-evaluation-and-mitigation-strategy)** · *Clinical & Safety*

An FDA-mandated safety programme for a drug whose risks need managing beyond its label, through prescriber certification, documented patient acknowledgement or restricted distribution.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/rems-risk-evaluation-and-mitigation-strategy)

**[Reporting Limit](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/reporting-limit)** · *Quality*

The lowest value a laboratory will put on a certificate, set at or above the limit of quantitation. Anything smaller is reported against that limit rather than as a number nobody can defend.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/reporting-limit)

**[Reproducibility](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/reproducibility)** · *Quality*

Agreement between results for the same material measured in different laboratories. It is the widest form of precision and the one that matters when a buyer sends a vial for independent testing.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/reproducibility)

**[Research Grade](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/research-grade)** · *Quality*

An unregulated commercial label, not a defined standard. What it means in practice is set by the specification and certificate behind it, and two suppliers using the phrase may be describing materially different material.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/research-grade)

**[Research Use Only (RUO)](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/research-use-only-ruo)** · *Regulatory*

A label stating that material is supplied for laboratory work and not for human or veterinary use. It is a category of supply rather than a certification of quality: on its own it carries no requirement that the contents be sterile, pure or correctly identified, which is why the certificate matters separately.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/research-use-only-ruo)

**[Residue](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/residue)** · *Chemistry & Structure*

One amino acid once it sits inside a chain, so called because the condensation that formed the bond left part of the free molecule behind. A peptide described as fifteen residues long has fifteen amino acids in it.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/residue)

**[Resin](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/resin)** · *Chemistry & Structure*

The insoluble polymer bead the chain is assembled on in solid-phase synthesis. Anchoring the chain to it is what lets excess reagents and by-products be washed away at every step, which is the entire advantage of the method.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/resin)

**[Retention Time](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/retention-time)** · *Quality*

How long a component takes to travel through the column and reach the detector. It is characteristic of a compound under fixed conditions, which is why identity is often confirmed by a sample and a reference standard eluting at the same time.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/retention-time)

**[Retest Date](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/retest-date)** · *Storage & Stability*

The date by which stored material should be re-examined against its specification rather than discarded. It is used where stability data support continued use after confirmation, and it is a different claim from an expiry date.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/retest-date)

**[Retraction](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/retraction)** · *Evidence & Research*

A journal's formal withdrawal of a published paper for error or misconduct. Retracted papers keep accruing citations for years, so a claim can stay in circulation long after its source was withdrawn.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/retraction)

**[Reversed-Phase HPLC (RP-HPLC)](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/reversed-phase-hplc-rp-hplc)** · *Quality*

The chromatographic mode almost all peptide purity work uses: a non-polar stationary phase with a polar moving phase, separating components by hydrophobicity. The gradient usually runs water and acetonitrile with an acidic modifier.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/reversed-phase-hplc-rp-hplc)

**[Ribosome](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/ribosome)** · *Cell Biology*

The machine that reads messenger RNA and assembles proteins from amino acids. Synthetic peptides bypass it entirely, which is why a synthetic sequence can contain D-amino acids and unnatural residues that no ribosome would incorporate.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/ribosome)

**[Rink Amide Resin](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/rink-amide-resin)** · *Chemistry & Structure*

A resin whose linker releases the chain as a C-terminal amide. Many research peptides are amidated at the C-terminus, and this is the usual way that is achieved.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/rink-amide-resin)

## S

**[S0 (Non-Approved Substances)](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/s0-non-approved-substances)** · *Anti-Doping*

The Prohibited List class covering any pharmacological substance not currently approved by any governmental health authority for human therapeutic use. It is a catch-all, and it captures most purely experimental research peptides by default.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/s0-non-approved-substances)

**[S2 (Peptide Hormones and Growth Factors)](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/s2-peptide-hormones-and-growth-factors)** · *Anti-Doping*

The Prohibited List class covering peptide hormones, growth factors, related substances and mimetics, including growth hormone, its releasing factors and secretagogues, IGF-1 and its analogues, and erythropoietin-receptor agonists. Prohibited at all times.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/s2-peptide-hormones-and-growth-factors)

**[S4 (Hormone and Metabolic Modulators)](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/s4-hormone-and-metabolic-modulators)** · *Anti-Doping*

The Prohibited List class covering hormone and metabolic modulators, which includes myostatin-pathway inhibitors and metabolic modulators such as AMPK activators and PPAR-delta agonists. Prohibited at all times.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/s4-hormone-and-metabolic-modulators)

**[Sample Identification](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/sample-identification)** · *Quality*

The unique reference a laboratory assigns to what it received, distinct from the lot number the supplier assigned. A certificate carrying both is traceable from the vial to the bench and back.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/sample-identification)

**[Sample Size](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/sample-size)** · *Evidence & Research*

How many participants a study enrolled, written as n. Small samples give unstable estimates and are the commonest reason an early positive result fails to replicate.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/sample-size)

**[SASP (Senescence-Associated Secretory Phenotype)](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/sasp-senescence-associated-secretory-phenotype)** · *Pharmacology*

The mixture of cytokines, chemokines, growth factors and proteases that senescent cells release. It is the reason a non-dividing cell is a problem rather than merely inert, and clearing the cells that produce it is the senolytic premise.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/sasp-senescence-associated-secretory-phenotype)

**[Satellite Cells](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/satellite-cells)** · *Cell Biology*

Muscle stem cells sitting between the sarcolemma and the basement membrane of a fibre. Damage or mechanical load activates them and they fuse to repair the fibre. MGF is studied for its role in that activation.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/satellite-cells)

**[Satiety](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/satiety)** · *Pharmacology*

The state of fullness that ends a meal and delays the next one, as distinct from the absence of hunger. Appetite-acting peptides are generally described in trials as increasing satiety rather than as suppressing hunger.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/satiety)

**[Secretagogue](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/secretagogue)** · *Pharmacology*

A compound that causes something else to be secreted. In peptide work the something else is almost always growth hormone, which is why the word usually appears as part of growth hormone secretagogue.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/secretagogue)

**[Senescent Cells](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/senescent-cells)** · *Pharmacology*

Cells that have stopped dividing for good but resist apoptosis and persist, secreting inflammatory mediators as they go. They accumulate with age and their secretions degrade the tissue around them.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/senescent-cells)

**[Senolytic](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/senolytic)** · *Pharmacology*

A compound meant to clear senescent cells selectively while leaving dividing ones alone. FOXO4-DRI is the peptide studied this way, in cell and animal models, and human data for the approach is minimal.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/senolytic)

**[Sequence](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/sequence)** · *Basics*

The order of the amino acids along a chain, written N-terminus first in one-letter or three-letter codes. The sequence is the compound's identity: two materials with different sequences are different substances whatever name is printed on the vial.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/sequence)

**[Serious Adverse Event](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/serious-adverse-event)** · *Clinical & Safety*

An adverse event that results in death, is life-threatening, requires hospitalisation, causes lasting disability or a congenital anomaly. The classification is about the outcome, not about how severe the experience felt.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/serious-adverse-event)

**[Shelf Life](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/shelf-life)** · *Storage & Stability*

How long a material stays within its specification under stated storage conditions. The figure is meaningless without the conditions attached, and it is established by stability study rather than estimated.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/shelf-life)

**[Side-Chain Protection](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/side-chain-protection)** · *Chemistry & Structure*

Protecting groups on amino acid side chains, as distinct from the one on the alpha-amino group removed at every cycle. Side-chain groups stay on throughout the synthesis and come off together at final cleavage.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/side-chain-protection)

**[Signal Peptide (Targeting Sequence)](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/signal-peptide-targeting-sequence)** · *Chemistry & Structure*

A short stretch at the start of a newly made protein that routes it to the right compartment and is then cleaved off. It is unrelated to a signaling peptide, which is a finished molecule that carries a message between cells; the two terms are a standing source of confusion.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/signal-peptide-targeting-sequence)

**[Signal Transduction](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/signal-transduction)** · *Signaling Pathways*

How a signal arriving at a cell surface becomes a change inside the cell: receptor binding, a relay of intracellular messengers, and an effect on enzyme activity or gene expression. Amplification at each stage is why a small number of bound receptors produces a large response.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/signal-transduction)

**[Signaling Peptide](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/signaling-peptide)** · *Basics*

A peptide whose function is to carry a message, binding a receptor to change what a cell does. Appetite, fluid balance and pituitary output are all regulated this way, by GLP-1, oxytocin and kisspeptin respectively.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/signaling-peptide)

**[Sirtuin](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/sirtuin)** · *Pharmacology*

A family of NAD-dependent enzymes that modify proteins involved in metabolism, DNA repair and stress response. They are the usual mechanistic hook for claims made about NAD precursors.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/sirtuin)

**[SNARE Complex](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/snare-complex)** · *Signaling Pathways*

The protein machinery, SNAP-25 with VAMP and syntaxin, that fuses transmitter vesicles with the cell membrane so their contents are released. Botulinum toxin cleaves SNAP-25; the peptide SNAP-8 is named for interfering with assembly of the same complex.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/snare-complex)

**[Solid-Phase Peptide Synthesis (SPPS)](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/solid-phase-peptide-synthesis-spps)** · *Chemistry & Structure*

The standard way peptides are made: the chain is anchored to a resin bead and extended one protected residue at a time, then cleaved off and purified. Coupling efficiency compounds over the chain, so yield and purity both fall as length rises.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/solid-phase-peptide-synthesis-spps)

**[Solubility](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/solubility)** · *Chemistry & Structure*

How much of a peptide a given solvent will take into solution. It is sequence-specific rather than general: some materials need a trace of acid or a small percentage of organic solvent before water will carry them, and forcing the issue with agitation causes aggregation instead.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/solubility)

**[Solution-Phase Synthesis](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/solution-phase-synthesis)** · *Chemistry & Structure*

Building a peptide in solution, with isolation and purification between steps rather than washing a resin. It is laborious for long chains and remains economic at scale for short ones.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/solution-phase-synthesis)

**[Somatostatin](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/somatostatin)** · *Pharmacology*

The hypothalamic hormone that brakes growth hormone release, acting as the counterweight to GHRH. The alternation between the two is what makes natural growth hormone secretion pulsatile rather than continuous.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/somatostatin)

**[Sonication](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/sonication)** · *Preparation*

Applying ultrasound to a liquid to speed dissolution or disperse aggregates. It appears in laboratory sample-preparation procedures, and prolonged or vigorous application can itself degrade a sensitive peptide.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/sonication)

**[Specification](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/specification)** · *Quality*

The full set of tests and acceptance criteria a material must meet to be released, agreed before manufacture. A certificate reports results; the specification is what makes those results a pass or a fail.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/specification)

**[Specified Substance](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/specified-substance)** · *Anti-Doping*

A Prohibited List category for substances more plausibly taken for a non-doping reason, which allows more flexibility in sanctioning. The peptide hormones in S2 are not specified, so the default first-violation sanction is the more severe one.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/specified-substance)

**[Stability](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/stability)** · *Storage & Stability*

Whether a material still meets its specification after storage, established by testing over time rather than assumed. A stability claim is only valid for the conditions it was measured under, which is why a temperature always accompanies it.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/stability)

**[Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/standard-operating-procedure-sop)** · *Quality*

A controlled document setting out how a task is performed, version-numbered and approved. Analytical results are defensible because an SOP existed and was followed, not because the analyst remembered the steps.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/standard-operating-procedure-sop)

**[Statistical Significance](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/statistical-significance)** · *Evidence & Research*

A judgement that a result is unlikely to have arisen by chance under the null hypothesis, conventionally at p below 0.05. It says nothing whatever about whether the difference is big enough to care about.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/statistical-significance)

**[Steady State](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/steady-state)** · *Pharmacology*

The point in repeated administration where the amount going in matches the amount being cleared, so peaks and troughs stop climbing. It takes roughly four to five half-lives to arrive, whatever the interval.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/steady-state)

**[Sterile Water](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/sterile-water)** · *Preparation*

Water that has been sterilised and contains no preservative. Nothing in it inhibits growth once the container is opened, which is the single difference from a preserved solvent and the reason the two are not interchangeable.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/sterile-water)

**[Sterility](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/sterility)** · *Quality*

The absence of viable microorganisms in a preparation. It is conferred by a process and preserved by a closure, so it is lost the moment either is compromised and nothing about the appearance of a vial reports that.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/sterility)

**[Sterility Testing](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/sterility-testing)** · *Quality*

Incubation of a sample in growth media to confirm no viable microorganism is present. Sterility is a property of a validated process and its container and is independent of chemical purity: a very pure material can be non-sterile and often is.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/sterility-testing)

**[Synthetic Peptide](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/synthetic-peptide)** · *Chemistry & Structure*

A peptide assembled chemically, in practice almost always by solid-phase synthesis, rather than extracted from tissue or expressed in cells. Research peptides are overwhelmingly synthetic, which is also why their identity can be checked against a known sequence.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/synthetic-peptide)

**[System Suitability](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/system-suitability)** · *Quality*

Checks run immediately before samples to prove the instrument is fit to measure anything that day: peak shape, resolution between critical pairs, and repeatability of replicate injections. A failed system suitability invalidates the run rather than the material.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/system-suitability)

**[Systematic Review](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/systematic-review)** · *Evidence & Research*

A review that follows a search and appraisal protocol written in advance, so which studies were included is reproducible rather than left to the author's discretion.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/systematic-review)

## T

**[Tachyphylaxis](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/tachyphylaxis)** · *Pharmacology*

Response falling away rapidly over a few administrations rather than gradually, which separates it from slower desensitization. Reports of it appear with melanocortin agonists and with some growth hormone releasing peptides.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/tachyphylaxis)

**[Tandem Mass Spectrometry (MS/MS)](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/tandem-mass-spectrometry-ms-ms)** · *Quality*

Selecting one ion, fragmenting it, and measuring the fragments. The fragment pattern is far more specific than mass alone, which is why certificates quote transitions rather than a single mass for confirmation.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/tandem-mass-spectrometry-ms-ms)

**[Telomerase](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/telomerase)** · *Pharmacology*

The enzyme that rebuilds telomeres and so offsets the shortening that comes with each division. Epithalon is studied for effects on its activity, with human cell-culture work from Khavinson and colleagues in 2003, rodent studies and Russian clinical reports, and no Western randomised trial.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/telomerase)

**[Telomere](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/telomere)** · *Cell Biology*

The repetitive DNA cap at the end of a chromosome, which shortens a little with each division. Telomere length is a popular and heavily disputed marker of biological ageing.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/telomere)

**[Testing Laboratory](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/testing-laboratory)** · *Quality*

The facility that performed the analysis, named on the certificate. Independence matters to what the certificate is worth: a result from a laboratory with no commercial interest in the material carries weight a supplier's internal figure does not.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/testing-laboratory)

**[Tetrapeptide](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/tetrapeptide)** · *Chemistry & Structure*

Four residues. Epithalon (Ala-Glu-Asp-Gly) and several of the bioregulators are tetrapeptides.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/tetrapeptide)

**[TFA Salt (Trifluoroacetic Acid)](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/tfa-salt-trifluoroacetic-acid)** · *Chemistry & Structure*

The counter-ion left behind by reversed-phase purification unless it is deliberately exchanged. Residual TFA affects cell-based assays and can be reported on a certificate, so a salt-form statement is worth reading rather than skipping.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/tfa-salt-trifluoroacetic-acid)

**[TGA (Therapeutic Goods Administration)](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/tga-therapeutic-goods-administration)** · *Regulatory*

Australia's regulator for therapeutic goods. It places peptides in scheduling categories, commonly Schedule 4, and sets the rules for importation, which differ from both the US and the EU positions.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/tga-therapeutic-goods-administration)

**[Therapeutic Use Exemption (TUE)](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/therapeutic-use-exemption-tue)** · *Anti-Doping*

Advance permission for an athlete to use a prohibited substance for a documented medical condition, granted against published criteria. It normally has to be in place before use rather than claimed after a positive test.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/therapeutic-use-exemption-tue)

**[Therapeutic Window](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/therapeutic-window)** · *Clinical & Safety*

The span between the smallest amount that produces an effect on the dose-response curve and the amount at which toxicity appears. A narrow window is what forces concentration monitoring in clinical use.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/therapeutic-window)

**[Third-Party Tested](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/third-party-tested)** · *Quality*

Analysed by a laboratory independent of the supplier. The phrase is only worth the certificate behind it: the useful questions are which laboratory, which lot, which tests, and whether the report can be verified at source.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/third-party-tested)

**[Third-Party Testing](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/third-party-testing)** · *Quality*

Analysis by a laboratory with no commercial interest in the result, as against in-house testing by the manufacturer or seller. The independence is the whole value, and it is only real if the laboratory is named and its report is reproducible.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/third-party-testing)

**[Tolerability](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/tolerability)** · *Clinical & Safety*

How well the unwanted effects of a compound are put up with, as distinct from how safe it is. Nausea rarely threatens anybody and routinely ends participation, which is why trials report discontinuation rates alongside adverse events.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/tolerability)

**[Triglycerides](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/triglycerides)** · *Clinical & Safety*

The main form fat is stored and transported in, measured in a standard lipid panel. It is reported as a metabolic endpoint and is sensitive to how recently the sample was taken relative to eating.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/triglycerides)

**[Tripeptide](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/tripeptide)** · *Chemistry & Structure*

Three residues joined by two peptide bonds. GHK, the copper-binding tripeptide, is the example most often met in this vertical.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/tripeptide)

**[Triple Agonist](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/triple-agonist)** · *Pharmacology*

One molecule that activates three receptors. GLP-3 RT acts at the GLP-1, GIP and glucagon receptors, and it is the compound that put the class into clinical development.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/triple-agonist)

**[Trityl (Trt)](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/trityl-trt)** · *Chemistry & Structure*

A bulky acid-labile protecting group used on cysteine, histidine, asparagine and glutamine side chains. Its sensitivity to mild acid is what makes selective removal possible while other groups stay in place.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/trityl-trt)

## U

**[Uptake](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/uptake)** · *Pharmacology*

Movement of a substance from outside a cell or compartment to inside it, whether passively or by a transporter. It is measured in a defined system, and an uptake result in cultured cells does not establish what happens in an organism.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/uptake)

**[Uronium Salt](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/uronium-salt)** · *Chemistry & Structure*

A family of fast coupling reagents including HBTU and HATU. They activate quickly and are used where a difficult coupling needs the help, at a higher cost than a carbodiimide.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/uronium-salt)

**[USP (United States Pharmacopeia)](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/usp-united-states-pharmacopeia)** · *Regulatory*

The non-governmental body that publishes public quality standards, called monographs, for drug substances and preparations. US law makes compliance with an applicable monograph a condition of several compounding pathways, and USP <797> covers sterile compounding.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/usp-united-states-pharmacopeia)

**[USP Chapter 797](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/usp-chapter-797)** · *Regulatory*

The United States Pharmacopeia chapter governing compounded sterile preparations, covering environment, training and beyond-use dating. It applies to pharmacies and compounding facilities, not to research supply.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/usp-chapter-797)

**[USP Grade](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/usp-grade)** · *Quality*

Material meeting the monograph for that substance in the United States Pharmacopeia, which specifies the tests and limits. The grade is only meaningful where a monograph exists, and most research peptides have none.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/usp-grade)

## V

**[Variability](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/variability)** · *Quality*

The spread in results from repeated measurement or from different units of the same material. Distinguishing variability in the method from variability in the product is the reason control samples are run alongside real ones.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/variability)

**[Vasopressin](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/vasopressin)** · *Pharmacology*

A nine-residue posterior-pituitary peptide, also called antidiuretic hormone, that governs water retention by the kidney and raises vascular tone. It differs from oxytocin at two residues, which is a standard illustration of how much a single substitution changes.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/vasopressin)

**[Vial](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/vial)** · *Preparation*

The sealed glass or plastic container a peptide is supplied in, closed with an elastomeric stopper under an aluminium crimp. A single-entry vial carries no preservative and is intended to be entered once; a multiple-entry vial carries one so it can be entered repeatedly.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/vial)

**[Visceral Adipose Tissue (VAT)](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/visceral-adipose-tissue-vat)** · *Pharmacology*

Fat held deep in the abdomen around the organs, as distinct from the layer directly under the skin. It is quantified by imaging rather than by a tape measure, and it is the endpoint the tesamorelin trials were built on.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/visceral-adipose-tissue-vat)

## W

**[WADA (World Anti-Doping Agency)](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/wada-world-anti-doping-agency)** · *Anti-Doping*

The body that publishes the World Anti-Doping Code and the annual Prohibited List adopted by Olympic sport and most national anti-doping organisations. Its rules run independently of whether a substance is an approved medicine anywhere.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/wada-world-anti-doping-agency)

**[Wang Resin](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/wang-resin)** · *Chemistry & Structure*

A resin whose linker releases the chain as a free carboxylic acid at the C-terminus. The choice of resin decides the C-terminal form, which is why it is picked before the first residue is attached.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/wang-resin)

**[Washout Period](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/washout-period)** · *Clinical & Safety*

The interval left for a compound to clear before something else begins, conventionally five half-lives. Crossover trials are built around it, because carry-over from the first arm would contaminate the second.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/washout-period)

**[Working Stock](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/working-stock)** · *Preparation*

A diluted solution prepared from a concentrated one for day-to-day use, so the concentrated stock is disturbed as rarely as possible. The dilution factor and the date belong on the label, because a working stock is the thing most likely to be misidentified later.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/working-stock)

**[Wound Healing](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/wound-healing)** · *Cell Biology*

The staged repair sequence of clotting, inflammation, new tissue formation and remodelling that follows injury. Animal wound models are the dominant evidence base for tissue-repair research peptides, and the staging is why a result at one time point says little about another.

[Read the full definition →](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/wound-healing)

## Y

## Z

**[Zwitterion](https://voltapeptides.com/glossary/zwitterion)** · *Chemistry & Structure*

A molecule carrying a positive and a negative charge at the same time while being neutral overall. Free amino acids exist as zwitterions at physiological pH, which is why they are far more water-soluble than their structures suggest.
